3. BACK-TRANSFORMS (Part B: the scoring side; no new forecast).  On the incumbent's forecasts of every class of Table 1
   (OLS on the HAR set; ridge, lasso, elastic net, LightGBM, XGBoost, the forest on the full and HAR sets: 13
   forecasts).  Every correction of bar t on trading session d uses only data of the sessions before d (lagged one
   session), except the contract's EWMA, which uses bars up to t - 1 (observed at the forecast origin, as the 2026-08
   contract).  Trailing window L = 250 sessions (the sessions of the forecast file, 2014-01-02 on; a session with no
   usable bar counts); usable bar = finite positive plain, finite B > 0, finite RV > 0.  Burn-in: with fewer than 20
   sessions of history the correction is the identity (plain); the window expands to 250 sessions.
     plain      pred_adj^2 x B (the paper's)
     c          c_d x plain, c_d = mean(RV / plain) over the window (the QLIKE-optimal constant, trailing)
     c_slot     c_{d,s} x plain, c_{d,s} = mean(RV / plain) over the window's bars of the bar's slot s (13 slots);
                identity where the slot has fewer than 20 usable bars
     duan_fit   Duan's smear on the fit scale: mean_i (pred_adj + e_i)^2 x B
                = (pred_adj^2 + 2 pred_adj ebar + mean e^2) x B,
                e = true_adj - pred_adj (true_adj = the winsorized target the model is fitted to), over the window
     duan_raw   the same with e = sqrt(RV / B) - pred_adj (the unwinsorized target; the smear that estimates
                E[RV | x] when e is independent of x)
     contract   the 2026-08 causal contract, made strictly causal on the final forecasts: m = a + b pred_adj with
                (a, b) the OLS of sqrt(RV / B) on [1, pred_adj] over the window (identity if fewer than 3 bars or a
                degenerate fit); s2_t = 0.6 s2_{t-1} + 0.4 e2_{t-1}, e2 = (sqrt(RV / B) - m)^2 of the previous usable
                bar (each residual enters once), seeded at the first corrected session with the window's mean squared
                MZ residual; forecast (m^2 + s2) x B.
     oracle_c   c* x plain with c* = mean(RV / plain) over the scored bars of the span: EX POST, NOT CAUSAL, the bound.
   A correction whose forecast is not finite and positive on a bar falls back to plain there (counted).

7. WHAT COUNTS AS "HELPS"
   Scorings: (P) plain, the variant's inverse map of section 2 without correction (the paper's convention); (C) the
   causal scalar c of section 3 applied to every variant alike (it removes the level bias that the maps differ in).
   For a transform T with leave-one-out variant V_T (V1 .. V8; V2 is "1/99 vs 5/95", V3 "sqrt vs log"), per class, on
   span all:
     "T helps in class k"   QLIKE(V_T) > QLIKE(V0) with DM p < 0.05 under both (P) and (C)
     "T hurts in class k"   QLIKE(V_T) < QLIKE(V0) with DM p < 0.05 under both (P) and (C)
     otherwise              "no robust effect" (each scoring's number reported)
   By how much: the QLIKE change of V0 against V_T in % of QLIKE(V_T) (the gain the transform buys), with the DM t and
   p, under (P) and (C), per class; counts over the classes of helps / hurts / no robust effect.  V0 in or out of each
   MCS.  ev, ord and the 16:00 spans are reported with the same statistics; no verdict rests on them alone (81 event
   days).  No multiplicity adjustment beyond the MCS.  The pre-sample (2013) numbers are reported and never enter a
   verdict.
   Part B: a correction "beats plain in class k" iff its QLIKE is lower with DM p < 0.05 on span all (and "loses" in
   the mirror case); reported per class (13 forecasts), with the QLIKE change in %, the same on ev, ord, c16, pb; no
   pooled verdict across classes.  oracle_c is the bound, never a competitor.

10. LATER PHASE (specified here, run only after the author's go): FIT TO THE EVALUATION LOSS
   The transforms exist to make an L2 fit on a heavy-tailed target behave; the loss the paper reports is QLIKE.  QLIKE
   is, per bar, half the gamma deviance of RV against the forecast (y / f - ln(y / f) - 1), so a fit that minimizes the
   gamma deviance with a log link estimates E[RV | x] directly, with no square-root map, no winsorization and no
   back-transform bias (Patton 2011: QLIKE is robust to noise in the RV proxy).
     trees     LightGBM objective "gamma" and XGBoost "reg:gamma" (log link) on y = RV / B of design V8's target
               (TARGET_LEVEL=1, TARGET_WINSOR=0) with the incumbent's features (the full set of V0); bars with RV = 0
               given weight 0 (gamma needs y > 0); forecast exp(score) x B; the frozen configurations, with
               n_estimators and learning_rate re-chosen on fold 3 only.
     linear    (i) y = log(RV / B) by L2 (design V3 without winsorization), forecast exp(pred) x B times the causal
               scalar c of section 3; (ii) the penalized gamma GLM with log link (IRLS, ridge penalty chosen on fold
               3), refit at the 250-solve cadence (no rank-one update exists).
   Scored as section 4 against V0 (plain and c) on the spans of section 5.
