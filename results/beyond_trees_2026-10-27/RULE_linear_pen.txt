RULE: the linear factor tests with PENALIZED estimators (ridge, lasso, elastic net), so that the linear and the trees'
recovery shares are on the same footing.  Written 2026-10-28 by the worker that runs it, committed and pushed before any
computation.  Label of every output: "requested by the author after the OLS factor tests; not pre-registered".

REQUEST (the author, 28 Oct, verbatim): "they're cheap, and they make the results apples to apples. run it."
Follow-up (the author, 28 Oct, verbatim, before this rule was committed): "how are we sure that the pca isn't
handicapping the factor model in comparison to the other ablations?"  -> the controls C1-C3 of section 6.

DISCLOSURE.  Requested after both OLS factor tests (channels RULE section 4, results/channels_2026-10-26/factor; this
folder's RULE_linear.txt with Amendment A, linear/) and the penalization split (linear/ADDENDUM_pen_split.md) had been
seen: beyond HAR + RM + FOMC the penalized classes' full sets do not beat their own penalized base on ORDINARY days
(ridge +0.15%, lasso +0.00%, elastic net -0.57%, none significant).  Not pre-registered in that sense; everything below
is fixed before any walk.

WHY (the mismatch): the earlier linear tests fit the base and the component arms by OLS but measure R against a
PENALIZED full set (the lower of Table 1's ridge / elastic net), so R mixed the components' contribution with the
penalty's; the trees' test (RULE.txt section 1) fits base, arms and full set with ONE model.  Here every linear class is
the one model of its own base, arms and full set: the estimator changes from OLS to the penalized classes.

1. TESTS (two, same code)
   A. BASE = the HAR benchmark set (12 columns: har_ma_1 .. har_ma_3125, DOW_0 .. DOW_4, hour).  BLOCK = the value
      averages adj_<s>_ma_k (k = 1 .. 3125) of the 43 non-FOMC series (258 columns; the realized moments folded in), the
      block of tab:rh_factor (experiments/factor_har_2026_10_26.block_names).
   B. BASE = the add_rm_fomc arm (132 columns: HAR set + the futures' realized moments + the FOMC group, value and
      availability averages; experiments/ablate_arms.arm_names("add_rm_fomc")).  BLOCK = the value averages of the 36
      series of ew, vw, liq, iv, sent, vd (216 columns), the trees' factor test's block
      (experiments/beyond_factor_linear.block_names).
   Design: Table 1's (13 regular-hours bars pooled, base-5 HAR on the 48-interval grid, W = 3,000 days = 39,000 rows,
   FOMC-patched design D13), the laptop rebuild in 12 column parts used by the OLS tests (scratchpad fl/design_parts,
   pool_linear_pretune.py design --part K/12); refit at every bar from Table 1's walk start (2013-12-27 16:00, 33,997
   solves); forecasts 2014-01-02 .. 2024-04-30.

2. COMPONENTS (exactly the existing tests'; unchanged)
   Once a trading day, from the window rows [t0 - W, t0) ending at the bar before the day's first bar t0, held for the
   day's bars (every training row of a bar's window and the bar's own row take the day's map): active block columns =
   window s.d. > 1e-12 and the series' k = 1 column has >= 250 rows off its window mode; PCA = eigenvectors of the active
   columns' window correlation matrix (descending; signed to a non-negative sum); PLS = SIMPLS weights of the
   standardized active block against the window's target.  Test A: experiments/factor_har_2026_10_26.day_map on the
   rolled window sums (its walk's route); test B: experiments/beyond_factor_linear.compute_map on the materialized window
   (its walk's route), here also returning every active eigenvector (C1).

3. ESTIMATORS (the change from OLS: Table 1's full sets and the ablation's add arms, ablation RULE section 3 and
   addenda F, G)
   ridge (alpha 1e3), lasso (alpha 1e-4), elastic net (alpha 1e-3, l1_ratio 0.5): the full set's frozen penalty
   (w3000 frozen_penalties.json all_features), penalty factor r = 0.01 on the HAR benchmark block (12 columns), 1 on
   every other column (the realized-moment and FOMC columns of base B, the component scores, the block's columns in C2:
   "the component scores are penalized as exogenous columns"); experiments/pool_linear_pretune.py's estimator semantics
   at its defaults (STANDARDIZE_PENALTY: the penalty on the window-standardized coefficients s_j b_j; intercept free;
   the identifiability mask at every 250th solve; the evidence mask; ridge with the s.d. of every bar's own window;
   lasso / elastic net with the s.d. held between cold seeds -- every 250th solve and the runner's data-driven reseeds
   (lasso mask additions, evidence, binomial availability rule) -- and the KKT-certified solution).
   - Implementation: the walk cannot use the runner's warm rank-one path (each day's loadings change every training
     row's scores), so it solves every bar's problem exactly (ridge: the linear system; lasso / elastic net: an
     active-set solve warm-started from the previous bar's support, certified by the runner's KKT conditions and
     tolerance, falling back to the runner's own batch solve) on the window Gram of [base, scores] (the Gram route of
     the OLS tests).  Masks and seed schedule of the base columns are tracked exactly as the runner; a KKT-triggered
     reseed of the runner (a numerical event of its warm path) is taken from the runner's own trace of the k = 0 walk if
     the gate G1 shows one (that walk then rerun locally for its trace), and applied to every arm of the class.
   - Score columns: never masked by the estimator's rules (they are continuous; their entry is section 2's active
     rule).  Weight (s.d.) of a score column: ridge, its window s.d. at every bar (as every ridge column); lasso /
     elastic net, its window s.d. at the later of the day's first bar (where the day's map defines it) and the last
     cold seed, held until either recurs (the runner's held-weight rule applied to a column redefined daily).
   - OLS (min-norm, Table 1's walk) is computed alongside as the reference class (gate G0, the C1 rotation gate), no
     reading.
   - BASE of each class (its OWN): test A, the class's HAR arm of Table 1 (window_sweep_2014start_fomcfix/w3000
     forecasts.parquet, predictor_set baseline; the HAR design is untouched by the patch), which carries the HAR set's
     OWN frozen penalty (ridge 1e-3, lasso 1e-8, elastic net 1e-7, r = 1); test B, the class's add_rm_fomc walk of the
     ablation (results/ablations_2014start_w3000/forecasts/linear_wide_rmfomc.parquet "<class>|add_rm_fomc"), which
     is the add-arm convention itself.  FULL of each class (its OWN): Table 1's patched full-set walk of the class
     (window_sweep_2014start_fomcfix/w3000, all_features).
   - Test A's k = 0 under the add-arm convention (the HAR set at the full set's penalty, r = 0.01) has no existing
     every-bar walk; it is walked here as the arm "k0" and reported with its R (the penalty switch alone; both penalties
     are tiny on the HAR block, so R(k0) ~ 0 is expected) -- the k-arms' R is read against the HAR arm (BASE A), and
     R(arm) - R(k0) is reported beside it as information.

4. ARMS (per test and class): k0; BASE + the first k PCA scores, k in {1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 20}; BASE + the first k PLS
   scores, k in {1, 2, 3} (Amendment A's sets); C1, C2 (section 6).  C3 on its sample days only.

5. GATES (before any scoring; gates.json of each test)
   G0  components = the existing tests': the OLS arms of this walk reproduce the earlier OLS factor tests' forecasts
       (test A: results/channels_2026-10-26/factor/forecasts.parquet; test B: linear/forecasts.parquet), every bar,
       1e-10 relative; test B's day maps (first 20 PCs + 3 PLS columns of P) equal the stored maps.npz (md5
       210da341ee6c73b2a3b71e361ab4a188) within 1e-10 relative to each column's norm.
   G1  k = 0 reproduces the class's existing walk to 1e-10 relative on every bar: test A, the walk code run with the HAR
       arm's own settings (its penalty, r = 1) against Table 1's HAR-arm walk of the class; test B, k0 against the
       ablation's add_rm_fomc walk of the class.  Target = Table 1's true_adj.
   G2  a direct solve equals the walk on the earlier tests' 7 spread days (2014-01-02, 2016-01-27, 2018-02-16,
       2020-03-16, 2020-03-17, 2022-04-06, 2024-04-30), first and last bar, every arm and class: the window materialized,
       the map from scratch (SVD PCA, NIPALS PLS, the entry rule from scratch), the scores materialized, the
       convention's weights from the materialized window (lasso / elastic net: the walk's held seed weights, which the
       check recomputes from the materialized seed window), the runner's own batch solve (pool_linear_pretune.fit_std /
       batch_z route); within 1e-8 relative.
   G3  scored set = Table 1's 33,527 bars, 81 EVENT / 2,498 ORDINARY days.
   GC1 (C1 rotation) OLS: C1 equals OLS on [BASE, the day's active block columns] to 1e-8 relative on every bar.
       Ridge: the add-arm convention standardizes every column, the scores included, so ridge's penalty on the full
       rotation is alpha sum_k lambda_k c_k^2 = alpha gamma' R gamma in the block's standardized coordinates (R the
       window correlation matrix) -- not isotropic, so ridge C1 is NOT a rotation of C2 under the convention (the
       coordinator's premise checked: false for ridge).  The rotation mechanics are gated instead on a variant with
       unstandardized scores (penalty alpha ||c||^2): at every day's first bar it equals ridge on [BASE, the day's active
       block columns] to 1e-8 relative.  Ridge / lasso / elastic net C1 under the convention: reported, no gate.

6. CONTROLS (the follow-up question: does the PCA construction handicap the factor arms against the ablations?)
   C1  FULL ROTATION: BASE + ALL active components of the day (k = the day's number of active block columns), each class.
   C2  VALUE-ONLY BLOCK: BASE + the block's value-average columns themselves (no PCA, no availability averages), the same
       estimator as an add arm (the estimator's own masks; its own data-driven seed events for lasso / elastic net).
       Its gap to the class's full set = what the components cannot see by construction (test B: the 36 series'
       availability averages; test A: the availability averages and the FOMC group); C2 - C1 = the component basis
       (the active rule and, for penalized classes, the standardization of the scores); C1 - k-arms = truncation.
   C3  LOADING FREQUENCY (information): 21 days at the quantiles i / 20, i = 0 .. 20, of the 2,579 evaluation days (the
       nearest day), plus 2020-03-16 if not among them: PCA k = 3 and 10 with the loadings recomputed at every bar (from
       the window ending at the previous bar) instead of once a day, every class; reported: max and mean relative
       forecast difference to the daily-loading arm, and QLIKE of both on those days' bars.
   Each reported next to the k-arms (QLIKE, % vs BASE, DM, R) in scores.csv and SUMMARY.md.

7. SCORING (as RULE.txt section 0 and the OLS tests)
   QLIKE on the bar's actual RV (plain = pred_adj^2 * B, B of Table 1's rows; rv_actual of the templates); spans
   ORDINARY (primary; the 81 scheduled FOMC statement days removed, channels RULE section 0), every bar, EVENT;
   Diebold-Mariano with the HLN correction on daily mean QLIKE, HAC lag 8 (src/evaluation/diebold_mariano.dm_test), of
   every arm against the class's BASE and against the class's FULL.  Per test, class and span:
   R_k = (L_BASE - L_k) / (L_BASE - L_FULL) with the class's own BASE and FULL.  Where the class's FULL does not beat
   its BASE on the span (L_FULL >= L_BASE) the span says "no gain to recover" and R is not reported as a share (the
   losses and DMs are).  FULL vs BASE (% change, DM, p) is reported for every class and span.

8. READING (per test and class, ORDINARY days; the earlier tests' rule, applied only where there is a gain)
   NO GAIN TO RECOVER if L_FULL >= L_BASE; else SHARED FACTOR if some k <= 3 (PCA or PLS) has R >= 0.75 and its loss is
   not significantly above FULL's (not: DM(arm vs FULL) > 0 with p < 0.05); else DENSE BEYOND A FEW FACTORS if R(PCA 10)
   < 0.5 and FULL beats BASE + 10 components significantly (DM(FULL vs pca10) < 0, p < 0.05); else INTERMEDIATE.  Where
   FULL beats BASE but not significantly (p >= 0.05), the reading carries "(gain not significant; R imprecise)".
   No reading is pooled over classes; the OLS versions and the trees' R are set beside them in SUMMARY.md, not merged.

AMENDMENT B (2026-10-28 ~19:35 PDT, before any scoring; the coordinator approved it as in line with the request)
(a) KKT-triggered reseeds.  Section 3 said such reseeds are "taken from the runner's own trace of the k = 0 walk ...
    (that walk then rerun locally for its trace)".  Found while gating: test B lasso k0 differs from the ablation's
    lasso add_rm_fomc walk by up to 7.8e-5 relative.  The mismatches start mid-block (e.g. solves 208 and 451 of the
    walk) and end at the next 250-solve tune, which is the signature of a KKT-triggered reseed of the runner's warm path.
    The local rerun of the runner itself (experiments/pool_linear_pretune.py walk, unchanged, the ablation's arguments,
    on a 132-column cache of the laptop's D13 rebuild) made 45 KKT reseeds of its own and differs from the ablation's
    walk by up to 3.3e-3 relative on 6,732 bars.  The warm path's KKT failures depend on the last bits of the design
    and of the BLAS, so a local trace does not reproduce the Hoffman2 walk's reseeds, and that walk's trace is not
    reachable from here.  Therefore the reseed solves of the class's existing k = 0 walk are identified from its
    forecasts:
    - walk k = 0 only; in each 250-solve block, the first solve whose forecast differs from the existing walk by more
      than 1e-10 relative is a candidate reseed (a cold seed on that solve's window: weights and masks recomputed, as
      the runner's _kkt_after -> _seed);
    - rerun with the candidates; a candidate is confirmed when the bars from it to the next seed then reproduce the
      existing walk to 1e-10;
    - iterate until G1 passes, or until a block's first mismatch does not resolve, which is then reported as a G1
      failure with its size.
    G1 for such a class is then a consistency check of the identified schedule, not an independent reproduction:
    every identified reseed must make all the following bars of its block match, which a wrong solve cannot do.
    The identified set is applied to every arm of the class (the k-arms, C1, C3 and C2), as section 3 says.  The same
    procedure is used for any class whose G1 shows this pattern (test A's HAR-arm walks <cls>_h; test B's ridge or
    elastic net).  Test A's add-arm arms (k0 and the k-arms) have no runner walk to take reseeds from, so none are
    applied there; the exact solve needs none, and a reseed only refreshes held weights (forecast effect <= ~1e-4
    relative, the size of the G1 mismatches above).
(b) C1 under the convention.  The add-arm convention standardizes every column, the scores included, so C1 is a
    whitened rotation: its penalty in the block's standardized coordinates is alpha gamma' R gamma, not isotropic (as
    GC1 already records).  Near-null directions of the collinear moving averages enter at unit scale and can blow up
    single forecasts (test A ridge: one bar, 2023-02-21 10:00, pred_adj -114 against k0's 0.85).  C1 is reported as
    defined: no trimming and no new arm, with its extreme bars stated (the count of bars where |pred_C1 / pred_k0 - 1|
    > 0.5, the minimum and the maximum, and the QLIKE without the bars where pred_C1 <= 0 as information only).

AMENDMENT C (2026-10-28 ~19:55 PDT, before any scoring; refines amendment B's identification, seen while gating).
In test B's lasso k0 two bars differ from the ablation's walk while the bars before and after agree to ~1e-13:
design row 61300 (6.7e-10 relative) and row 80804 (3.1e-9).  A KKT reseed would change every later bar of its block.
A one-bar difference is instead the runner's warm solution at that bar, accepted by its KKT check (tolerance 1e-9
max|c|) slightly off the exact optimum this walk solves for.
- Identification: a reseed candidate is the first solve of a SUSTAINED mismatch (that bar and the next bar of the
  block both above 1e-10), never an isolated bar.  Taking the two isolated bars as reseeds made their blocks worse,
  so they were dropped.
- G1: such isolated bars (both neighbours within 1e-10) are counted separately and listed in gates.json.  G1 passes
  when every other bar is within 1e-10 and every isolated bar is within 1e-8.

AMENDMENT D (2026-10-28 ~20:05 PDT, before any scoring; seen in test A's gates).
GC1's OLS check fails on a few bars: 5 bars above 1e-8 and 27 above 1e-10 out of 33,997; the median is 2.9e-14 and
the maximum 227 relative, at 2023-02-21 10:00.
- Cause: the day map of 2023-02-21 admits 4 new block columns.  These are series that pass the 250-row evidence rule
  that day, the window ending at the Presidents' Day session of 2023-02-20.  Their moving averages are nearly
  collinear in the window: the smallest correlation eigenvalues are 1.6e-11, 4.9e-10 and 1.3e-8, against a median
  minimum over days of about 2e-4.
- OLS's min-norm cut (eigenvalues below 1e-15 of the largest) then drops different directions in the score basis and
  in the raw-column basis.  The forecast row lies off the window's span in those directions.  The two forecasts are
  different min-norm answers to an ill-posed extrapolation, not a rotation error.
- Ridge's rotation check, which has no rank cut, holds to 8.5e-15 on every day's first bar, 2023-02-21 included.
- Therefore GC1's OLS check is reported (its bars above 1e-8, each with that day's smallest eigenvalue) and does not
  block scoring; the ridge rotation check stays a gate.
- The same near-null directions explain C1's extreme bars under the convention (amendment B (b)): the whitened C1
  scales them up to unit variance.  This only flags C1 on these days; the k-arms are unaffected, since their scores
  are the leading components.
