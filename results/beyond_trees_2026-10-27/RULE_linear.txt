DESIGN (as RULE.txt sections 0-1, linear)
- Table 1's design (13 bars pooled, base-5 HAR on the 48-interval grid, W = 3,000 days = 39,000 rows, FOMC-patched
  design D13), forecasts 2 January 2014 to 30 April 2024, OLS (min-norm, Table 1's walk) re-estimated at every bar.
- BASE_LIN = OLS on the 132 columns of the add_rm_fomc arm (HAR set + the futures' realized moments + the FOMC group).
- Components: exactly the tree version's construction (CARC addendum 1): the 216 value-average columns of the series of
  ew, vw, liq, iv, sent, vd; standardized within the window with the linear factor test's active-column rule; loadings
  recomputed once a trading day from the window ending at the bar before the day's first bar and held for that day;
  PCA = eigenvectors of the window correlation matrix; PLS = SIMPLS against the window's target.
- Arms: OLS on [BASE_LIN columns, first k scores], PCA k in {1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 20}, PLS k in {1, 2, 3} (Amendment A:
  the earlier linear factor test's sets).
- Gates before scoring: (G1) k = 0 reproduces the ablation's OLS add_rm_fomc forecasts to 1e-10 relative on every bar;
  (G2) the Gram-route forecast equals a direct lstsq on materialized scores within 1e-8 on at least 5 spread days;
  (G3) scored set = Table 1's 33,527 bars, 81 EVENT days / 2,498 ORDINARY days (channels RULE section 0).

SCORING (RULE.txt section 0): QLIKE on the bar's actual RV (plain = pred_adj^2 * B); every bar, ORDINARY (primary),
EVENT; DM with HLN on daily means, Newey-West lag 8 (src/evaluation/diebold_mariano.dm_test).
- FULL_LIN = the lower ORDINARY-day QLIKE of the patched full-set ridge and elastic net (Table 1).
- R = (L_BASE_LIN - L_arm) / (L_BASE_LIN - L_FULL_LIN), as in the earlier linear test. Reported alongside, as
  information only: FULL_LIN vs BASE_LIN on ORDINARY days (% change, DM, p).

READING (ORDINARY days; the earlier linear test's rule verbatim, Amendment A)
- SHARED FACTOR if some k <= 3 (PCA or PLS) has R >= 0.75 and its loss is not significantly above FULL_LIN's;
  DENSE BEYOND A FEW FACTORS if R(PCA 10) < 0.5 and FULL_LIN beats BASE_LIN + 10 components significantly;
  otherwise INTERMEDIATE.
- Descriptive, as the earlier test: mean share of the block's variance in components 1-3, and the first component's
  loadings (and the first PLS component's weights) summed by predictor group.
