# Independent audit of the macro release calendars (addendum L), 30 Oct 2026

Requested by the author on 30 Oct ("Before concluding only FOMC matters: run an independent audit (parallel read-only
subagents) that the macro release computations are correct"). Five read-only agents, each with a brief that carried no
conclusions; none changed a repo file. Their scratch evidence was in the session's temp folders
(`mac_audit_{dates,features,matrix,scoring,mechanism}`); the findings are recorded here.

| Part | Question | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Release dates and times against the official calendars | Correct in the training and evaluation span (2002-2024) |
| 2 | Features on release days, lookahead, equivalence with FOMC | Correct; no lookahead; FOMC's construction except one deliberate rule |
| 3 | Columns reach the model matrices | Yes, for trees and linear models; nothing drops or neutralizes them |
| 4 | Scoring spans, DM tests, FOMC's result reproduced | Correct; FOMC's committed numbers reproduced exactly |
| 5 | Mechanism: why the calendars add little | The pre-open release effect is already in the inputs at the first origin |

## 1. Dates and times
- CPI, employment, PPI: the BLS release archive, 807 dates 2002-2024, exact match. Trade: ALFRED and all 125 BEA release
  pages 2014-2024, exact. Retail: 254 Census advance-report PDFs, all match. GDP advance: all 47 BEA pages 2013-2024 and
  ALFRED, all match. Every entry of the correction lists (MISSING 23, OMITTED 10, MISDATED 4, NEXT_RELEASE 6,
  RESCHEDULED 27) confirmed against BLS, BEA, Census, OMB and contemporaneous reports.
- Release times: every release in 2014-2024, the correction lists and the reschedules was at 08:30 ET (BLS schedule
  pages 2002-2024, BEA and Census release headers). The only off-time release found is PPI of 1999-01-12 at 17:00,
  before every training window.
- Bar mapping: bar ends are naive ET with DST; a 08:30 release lands in the bar ending 09:00 (proved on April 2024
  minute data; RV traces on DST-adjacent days; employment days peak in the 09:00 bar on 295 of 312).
- Two notice times in MACRO_RESCHEDULED are assumed at 17:00 where the source gives no hour (BEA 2013-10-21, Census
  2019-02-28); possible lookahead of a few hours, training data only.
- Outside scope, queued (Q-2026-10-30-03): the FOMC calendar flags the 14:30 bar on 8 statement days of 2011-2012 whose
  statement came out at 12:30 ET (training windows only).

## 2. Features
- An independent recomputation of all 18 series matches on all 335,105 grid rows, NaN placement included. On every
  release date every bar has day = 1 and no other date does; since_inv = until_inv = 1 exactly on the 09:00 bar.
- Lookahead: 41,782 cut bars rebuilt from the frame known at t, 125,346 comparisons, 0 failures.
- Availability: until_inv is missing only in the 2018-19 shutdown runs (GDP 392, trade 340, retail 529 RTH rows, as
  RULE (c)); no live date zeroed.
- FOMC equivalence: fed the FOMC calendar, the macro builder reproduces day and until bit for bit; since differs on
  2020-03-15..04-29 because FOMC_SCHEDULE_2020_FIX restarts FOMC's counter at the emergency statement, while the macro
  counter counts from the last actual release (RULE (c)).
- After scaling, the 2018-19 shutdown gives four macro columns robust-IQR outliers of 15-25 in Feb-May 2019 (the
  build's feature-health check flags them; FOMC's columns peak at 6.0). Causal and as specified; a robustness score
  excluding 2019-01-22..06-14 is to be reported with the results.

## 3. Model matrices
- Every macro arm selects exactly its intended columns (names, counts, cache order) for trees and linear walks; no
  constant drop, NaN rule, mask or penalty removes the macro value columns. Ridge keeps all live macro columns; LightGBM
  and XGBoost split on about half of them.
- 72 of the 216 macro columns are constant 1 by construction (availability averages of the day and since series; FOMC
  has 18 of 36 the same way), so the usable set is smaller than the count suggests.
- No gate checks that the macro columns change a forecast; an assertion is to be added at the final merge.

## 4. Scoring
- rel, quiet / nrel, rel_<r>, ord and ev equal independently rebuilt day sets (590 / 1,932 / per release 122, 120, 124,
  42, 122, 123; 81 FOMC days; 2,579 days, 33,527 bars).
- The DM on the macro spans runs through the same code as on FOMC's day types; 2,994 DM rows recomputed independently
  (max difference 1e-13).
- The scratch run reproduces the committed scores and all 4,488 paper macros; HAR + FOMC's event-day gain appears
  through the same path, and placebo shifts of the event days remove it.
- 24 release days are also FOMC days and carry part of the per-release results (GDP: 5 of 42); the per-release overlap
  counts are now in the table note. p-values use the normal reference at small T (FOMC's event days included; queued,
  Q-2026-10-30-01).

## 5. Mechanism
- 53% of the release-day excess variance (08:30-16:00) is in the 08:30-09:00 release bar, which is not scored; the
  scored bars are 1.1-1.2x quiet days.
- At the 09:30 origin the HAR and realized-moment inputs already carry the release and slightly over-carry it; HAR + RM +
  FOMC leaves no systematic release-day under-forecast. FOMC is the opposite: the 14:00 statement falls inside a scored
  bar whose origin precedes it, and the inputs are below normal at that origin.
- The training target's winsorization clips the 14:30 bar on 64% of FOMC days (queued, Q-2026-10-30-02).
