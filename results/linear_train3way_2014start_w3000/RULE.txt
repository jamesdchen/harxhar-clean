Question: how does training on all 48 half-hour periods (ALL48), on the 13 regular-hours bars (RTH13), or on the
closing half hour only (CLOSE) compare, scored on the 13 RTH bars and on the closing half hour?  Feeds a decision on
whether the paper should score QLIKE and its tests on the close only.  Nothing under writeup/ is edited; no Overleaf sync.

2. ARMS (the training sample; everything else as the template)
   ALL48   every interval of the 48-interval grid (runner --sample 48, segment none): overnight, Sunday-evening and
           exchange-holiday intervals and the NYSE early-close sessions included, as the 48-interval sample has always
           been built.  Window 3,000 x 48 = 144,000 intervals (the pipeline's row count; about 2,950 sessions, since
           Friday and Sunday rows are partial days).
   RTH13   the template.  Its forecasts (w3000/forecasts.parquet) are reused, conditional on the gate (section 7).
   CLOSE   the closing half hour only: the 16:00 rows of the RTH13 design, taken from the RTH13 design caches rebuilt
           by this branch (gate (i)), so X, y and B of every 16:00 row are byte-identical to RTH13's; window 3,000 rows
           (sessions).  New code: experiments/linear_train3way.py derive_close (no other change to the rows).
   Secondary arms (labelled as such; not the comparison's primary rows):
   CLOSE_NAT  the closing half hour with the design built natively on the closing rows (runner --sample close =
           segment bar1600 of src/backtest/segmentation.py, lag scope global as rth13, the template's flags), i.e.
           the scaler and its guards on the closing rows; window 3,000 rows.
   ALL48@16, RTH13@16  ALL48 and RTH13 with the penalty re-selected on fold 3's validation rows ending 16:00 only
           (validation MSE at 16:00), to separate "pooled sample" from "tuned for the close".  Walked only for the
           models whose pick changes (else identical to the primary).  Kept only if it costs < 2x the primary (it
           cannot cost more than the primary: at most every model is walked once more).

3. PREDICTORS (the template's construction in every arm: src/backtest/executor.py through the spec's run_executor)
   HAR ladder base 5 (k = 1, 5, 25, 125, 625, 3125 intervals) and every trailing average computed on the full
   48-interval grid; HAR benchmark set = 6 HAR + calendar; full set = HAR + 47 series x 6 value averages + 47 x 6
   availability averages + calendar (RTH13: 12 and 564 columns).  The target adj_RV and its diurnal baseline B are
   built on the full grid before any slicing, so they are the same at every common row (asserted when scoring).
   Column differences, all necessary:
     ALL48: + is_overnight (DROP_OVERNIGHT_INDICATOR=0): 13 and 565 columns.
     CLOSE, CLOSE_NAT: the RTH13 columns; hour is constant on the closing rows and is removed by the runner's
       identifiability mask at every seed (as any column constant in a window).
   Sample-dependent steps (a necessary difference the brief did not list): the pipeline's rolling robust scaler
   (median / IQR over the trailing W rows of the arm's own sample, src/features/transforms/scaling.py), its guards
   (_build_scale_guards: reference IQR, MA-window erosion) and LATE_START_WARMUP's zeroing (W live rows) act on the
   rows of the design they are built on.  Raw (pre-scale) values of a 16:00 row are the same in every arm; scaled
   values are identical in RTH13 and CLOSE (CLOSE takes RTH13's rows) and differ in ALL48 and CLOSE_NAT through the
   scaler's statistics.  Quantified on the 16:00 rows of 2014-2024 at scoring (diagnostic only, not a gate).
   Runner-side masks evaluated on each arm's own fit window: identifiability (constant / duplicated columns),
   STANDARDIZE_PENALTY's evidence mask (>= 250 rows off the window mode) and the binomial availability rule; a
   3,000-row window (CLOSE) can therefore leave out more sparse columns.  Early-close sessions: dropped in RTH13,
   CLOSE, CLOSE_NAT (DROP_EARLY_CLOSE_SESSIONS, segment path), kept in ALL48 (the 48-interval path keeps every row).

4. TUNING (fold 3 only, the orchestrator's fold scheme counted in each arm's rows; PPD = 48, 13, 1)
   validation = the 250 x PPD rows before the first 2014-01-02 row (about calendar 2013), embargo 25 x PPD rows,
   fit = the W rows before the embargo.  ALL48: 12,000 / 1,200 / 144,000; RTH13: the template's (3,250 / 325 / 39,000,
   validation 2013-01-02 15:30 .. 2013-12-31 16:00); CLOSE, CLOSE_NAT: 250 / 25 / 3,000.  Criterion: the lowest mean
   validation MSE on the adjusted scale over the arm's own validation rows.  Grids: ridge 1e-2 .. 1e3, lasso and
   elastic net 1e-6 .. 1e-2 (l1_ratio 0.5), one value a decade, plus the grid rule (a pick at a grid end adds the next
   decade until interior; stop when flat within 1e-6 relative or after 6 decades).  One penalty a model, frozen over
   the evaluation span.  OLS: min-norm least squares (--ols auto), no penalty.  ALL48@16 / RTH13@16: the same fold.npz
   residuals restricted to the validation rows ending 16:00, the grid rule on that criterion (its extensions
   pretuned under _work/pretune16/).

5. MODELS AND WALK
   OLS (HAR set); ridge, lasso, elastic net on the HAR set and on the full set; solver settings unchanged
   (STANDARDIZE_PENALTY, WALK_KKT_RESEED, penalty factors).  Walk: the runner unchanged, refit at every row of the
   arm's sample from the 250-solve boundary at or before the first 2014-01-02 row to the end of the data (30 April
   2024): rank-one / warm homotopy update every row, identifiability mask and cold seed every 250th solve, window of
   the last W rows.  ALL48 also refits at overnight rows (the runner rolls its window one row at a time; there is no

   set: trailing averages through the bar ending 15:30).  Forecast of a row = the model fit on the W rows before it;
   plain = pred_adj^2 x B.  Chunks of 250-solve blocks (each chunk with a W-row halo reproduces the whole walk).

ADDENDUM A, 2026-10-23 22:07 PDT (2026-10-24 05:07 UTC), before any of its jobs: EQUAL TRAINING ROWS
   Owner (James, relayed by the coordinator): "we should also ablate equal training rows instead of fixing the raw
   clock time 3k day window."  The equal-calendar arms above run unchanged; this adds an equal-rows ablation.
   Arms (N = training rows; the window is the last N rows of the arm's sample at every origin):
     N = 3,000:  ALL48_N3k (about 62.5 days of 48 intervals), RTH13_N3k (about 231 sessions of 13 bars), CLOSE (the
                 existing arm: 3,000 closes = 3,000 sessions; reused, not rerun).
     N = 39,000: ALL48_N39k (about 812.5 days) against RTH13 (the existing 3,000-day arm, 39,000 rows; reused).
                 CLOSE cannot reach 39,000 rows.
   Exactly N rows, not a whole-day window: the runner takes a row-count window in its design-cache modes (new runner
   option --window-rows N in experiments/linear_train3way_runner.py; the runner's W is the only window cached_backtest,
   the walk and the pretune use; the spec's TRAIN_WIN in days is read by the design path only, which these arms never
   run).  So ALL48_N3k, RTH13_N3k and ALL48_N39k have windows of exactly 3,000, 3,000 and 39,000 rows.
   Design (experiments/linear_train3way.py derive_window): each new arm uses its equal-calendar sibling's design
   cache (ALL48 or the rebuilt RTH13) with the leading W_sibling - N rows dropped, so X, y, B and every date are the
   sibling's, byte for byte; only the regression window differs.  Columns, flags, models, solver settings (penalty
   factors, STANDARDIZE_PENALTY, WALK_KKT_RESEED), refit cadence (every row; mask and cold seed every 250th solve) and
   the walk's first origin (the same 250-solve boundary in the sibling's OOS index) are the sibling's.
   Scaler and late-start rule (the check asked for): both keep the sibling's 3,000-day window, i.e. the sibling's
   design matrix is used as it is.  Reason: the pipeline sets the rolling robust scaler's window (and LATE_START_WARMUP's
   "W live rows") to the training window only because it prescales with train_win; at 62.5-231 days that window would
   be about as short as, or shorter than, the longest trailing averages (k = 3,125 intervals of the 48-interval grid
   = about 65 days, in both the 48-interval and the 13-bar designs), so the median / IQR of the long HAR and
   value averages inside the window would be near-degenerate (the scaler's only floor on the target HAR columns is 1%
   of their running maximum IQR), and the late-start zeroing would end years earlier than in the siblings.  Keeping
   them makes the equal-rows arms differ from their siblings in the number of training rows only.  (The runner-side
   masks - identifiability, STANDARDIZE_PENALTY's evidence mask, the binomial availability rule - act on each fit
   window as always, so a 3,000-row window can leave out more sparse or constant columns, as in CLOSE.)
   Tuning: re-tuned with the native rule on fold 3 at that window: validation = the same 250 x PPD rows before the
   first 2014-01-02 row (about 2013) as the sibling, embargo 25 x PPD rows, fit = the N rows before the embargo; the
   lowest mean validation MSE (adjusted scale, the arm's own validation rows); the same grids and grid rule; frozen.
   No close-tuned (@16) variant for the new arms.
   Scoring as section 6: every RTH bar for ALL48_N3k, RTH13_N3k, ALL48_N39k; 16:00 for all; DM at equal N: at 16:00
   ALL48_N3k vs RTH13_N3k, CLOSE vs RTH13_N3k, CLOSE vs ALL48_N3k, ALL48_N39k vs RTH13; every bar ALL48_N3k vs
   RTH13_N3k, ALL48_N39k vs RTH13; also each equal-rows arm vs its equal-calendar sibling (16:00 and every bar); full
   vs HAR within each arm; the close D6 Sharpe ratio and the same pairs' differences (section 8).  One scoring job
   covers both designs (it replaces the equal-calendar-only export / score jobs 13361444 / 13361445, cancelled before
   they ran).
   Confounds each design leaves: equal calendar (3,000 days) = unequal rows (144,000 / 39,000 / 3,000); equal rows =
   unequal span and recency: at N = 3,000 the pooled windows cover only the last 62.5 (ALL48) or 231 (RTH13) days
   while CLOSE covers about 12 years; at N = 39,000 ALL48 covers about 812 days, RTH13 3,000.  In both designs the
   scaler and late-start warm-up are those of the 3,000-day designs.
   Compute estimate for the addendum: ALL48_N3k about 3-5 CPU-hours (127,000 origins a model, cheap seeds),
   ALL48_N39k about 4-6 (RTH13's per-row cost with a 39,000-row window, 3.7x the origins), RTH13_N3k under 1; with the
   equal-calendar arms (ALL48 10-20, ALL48@16 at most as much) the total stays well under 100 CPU-hours.
