4. WALK
   The forecast at an origin (design row r, position j of r in the arm's sample S) is fitted from scratch on the
   sample rows S[j - W] .. S[j - 1] with the fixed seed and predicts row r; plain = pred_adj^2 x B.  No state carries
   from one refit to the next (each fit is a new estimator; the templates' pieces resume mid-walk bit for bit), so
   refitting only at the origins gives exactly the forecast a walk refitting at every sample row would give there;
   verified by gate 2 before any 48-interval walk.  Origins:
     the 33,970 bar ends of the templates (every 13-bar design row from 2014-01-02 10:00 to 2024-04-30 16:00); in the
       48-interval design the rows with those end stamps (asserted present).  The ALL48 arms do not refit at their
       other rows (overnight, weekend, holiday rows and the early-close sessions' bars, none of which is scored).
     CLOSE: the 16:00 rows among them (one a session).
   Code: experiments/trees_train3way.py walk (pooled_trees_rerun_13's model code, checkpoints and meta; pieces of
   contiguous trading-day blocks, the templates' rule: n = ceil(projected wall hours / 3 h) from the time probe), merge
   (complete piece sets; one CPU family for rf_skx).  Environments: the templates' (LightGBM / XGBoost harxhar-r13,
   the forest venv_skx), read only.  Threads: LightGBM / XGBoost 8 (the templates'); the forest 8 in CLOSE and the N3k
   arms, 20 in ALL48_N39k and ALL48 (the rf_skx gate (d): 20 = 8 bit for bit); every piece's meta records them.
   CPU: CARC EPYC 7513 / 7542 only (--constraint), the templates' nodes.
