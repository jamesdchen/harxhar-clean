WALK_KKT_RESEED: after every warm online update of the lasso / elastic net, check the KKT conditions of the current
window's problem (residual r = c - G.theta with the penalty factors and L2 term; active j: r_j = mu_j s_j; inactive:
|r_j| <= mu_j) at the tolerance the batch certification `_kkt_ok` uses; if they fail, replace the warm solution by the
cold seed (the existing KKT-certified batch solve on the current window) and continue warm from it.

Gate: module flag WALK_KKT_RESEED (default True; off = the current walk bit for bit).  The number of KKT-triggered
reseeds is recorded in the walk's trace / log.

Implementation reading (fixed now, before any run):
  * "every warm online update" = every roll() of the regressor that went through the two enet_online rank-1 calls
    (add the entering row, remove the leaving row), i.e. the window has moved one row; the check is made at the next
    solve(), before that solve's forecast (MultiStageBacktest order: roll, solve, predict_one).  Not checked: the
    tune-boundary solves (they ARE the cold seed: mask recomputed, batch solve) and the rolls that took the spec's
    between-tune mask-addition reseed (also a cold seed) -- their KKT residual is recorded, not acted on.
  * "the current window's problem" = the warm state's own sufficient statistics, (Gr, c, mu_vec) of the regressor:
    Gr = Xa'Xa + lam2 pf_j on the diagonal of the non-locked columns (the L2 term), c = Xa'y, mu_vec = n alpha l1 pf_j
    (0 on the locked intercept), masked columns zero -- the matrices the homotopy itself maintains rank-one (they equal
    the exact window statistics up to the rank-one round-off that the cold seed every 250 solves re-anchors).
    r = c - Gr theta; active (theta_j != 0): |r_j - mu_j sign(theta_j)| <= tol; inactive: |r_j| <= mu_j + tol.
  * tol = 1e-9 * max(max_j |c_j|, 1)  -- the formula of _kkt_ok's caller enet_coef_pf (experiments/pool_linear_pretune.py),
    applied to this problem's c.
  * the cold seed = the regressor's own _seed() on the current window at the penalty and mask in force (FWL on the
    locked block + the batch elastic net, KKT-certified / repaired when pf != 1); the mask is not recomputed (un-masking
    stays a tune-boundary event, as the spec's between-tune additions).  The walk then continues warm from it; the
    250-solve tune schedule is unchanged.
  * ridge / OLS: closed form, never checked (asserted unchanged).
  * recorded per walk: number and solve indices of KKT-triggered reseeds, the violation before and after each, the
    post-check violation at every lasso / enet solve, the seconds spent in the check and in the reseeds.
