3. SCORING (experiments/all48_score.py; both parts; the paper's conventions, experiments/ablate_score.py)
   Forecast plain = pred_adj^2 x B (the design's own B).  Truth: the interval's sumret2 (data/core_stats.parquet), RV > 0.
   Loss QLIKE = r - ln r - 1, r = RV / plain (primary), and squared error (MSE x 1e10).
   Evaluation days E: the 2,579 days of Table 1 (NYSE days in 2014-01-02 .. 2024-04-30 with a positive 16:00 print,
   early closes dropped; ablate_score.evaluation_days).  An interval belongs to the calendar date of its bar-end stamp
   (ET; ablate_score.daily's grouping), so a day's 48 intervals end 00:00, 00:30, .., 23:30.
   Scored set of a span: its intervals with RV > 0 and a finite positive plain of every model of the part's panel.
   Spans:
     all48       every interval of the days in E
     rth13       end stamps 10:00 .. 16:00 of the days in E (Table 1's 33,527 bars when the panel covers them; recorded)
     non_rth35   the other 35 end stamps of the days in E (00:00 .. 09:30 and 16:30 .. 23:30): the overnight table
     clk_HHMM    each of the 48 end stamps alone (per interval)
     grid, grid_non_rth (secondary)  every grid row dated 2014-01-02 .. 2024-04-30 (Sunday evenings, exchange holidays'
                 sessions and early-close days included), and its non-RTH rows
   Per span and model: bars, days, QLIKE, MSE x 1e10.  Tests: Diebold-Mariano (src/evaluation/diebold_mariano.dm_test:
   HLN, Bartlett, Newey-West lag max(h - 1, floor(4 (T / 100)^(2/9)))) on the daily average loss (all48, rth13,
   non_rth35, grid spans) or the day's single loss (clk_HHMM); QLIKE (primary) and squared error.  Pairs: full vs HAR
   benchmark within each class (ridge, lasso, elastic net, LightGBM, XGBoost, forest), per span and per interval;
   secondary: each full set vs the OLS HAR.  Negative = the full set lower.  % vs HAR = 100 (QLIKE_full / QLIKE_HAR - 1).
   No correction for the 48 per-interval tests (exploratory; stated in every table).  Context only, no test across
   designs: Table 1's RTH numbers and, for the linear models, Part 1 against Part 2 (the FOMC fix, the walks otherwise
   alike).
   Outputs: metrics.csv, dm.csv, per_interval.csv, checks.json per part.
