GATE (before any SHAP number is read)
- G1 LightGBM, XGBoost: f_k(x_{r_k}) equals the walk's pred_adj at r_k BIT FOR BIT (float64 ==) at all 126 origins.
  The walk's rows come from the CARC W = 3,000 walk pieces (/scratch1/jc_905/trees_final/runs/w3000/pieces),
  only the 126 origin rows, fetched in one transfer once the pieces hold them.  If the laptop's build of the same
  library versions (Windows wheel vs the Linux wheel the walk ran) does not reproduce bit for bit, the gate is
  reported FAILED with the maximum absolute and relative differences and the coordinator is told before any result is
  interpreted and before any cluster use.
- G2 random forest: bit equality with the rf_skx walk is not expected (oneDAL's results differ across CPU families;
  the walk runs on EPYC 7513, the laptop is an Intel i7; the benchmark measured 3e-15 relative between EPYC and Xeon).
  (a) self-consistency: 3 origins (k = 1, 63, 126) refitted twice give bit-identical forecasts and SHAP values;
  (b) the oneDAL prediction equals the mean of the estimators_ tree predictions (the trees SHAP explains) to
  <= 1e-12 relative at every forecast row; (c) where the rf_skx W = 3,000 walk has the origin rows when fetched,
  the max relative difference is reported (agreement <= 1e-12 relative); otherwise (a) and (b) are the gate.
- G3 additivity at every forecast row: |phi_0 + sum_j phi_ij - f_k(x_i)| <= 1e-9 (LightGBM, RF; double precision)
  and <= 1e-5 x max(1, |f_k(x_i)|) (XGBoost computes in float32); interaction rows sum to the SHAP values to the same
  tolerances.

SHAP
- TreeSHAP, feature_perturbation = "tree_path_dependent", on the adjusted (model output, pred_adj) scale; exact.
  Why: (1) it is the TreeSHAP both boosting libraries compute natively and exactly (LightGBM pred_contrib, XGBoost
  pred_contribs / pred_interactions); (2) SHAP interaction values (M5) exist only for it; (3) it needs no background
  sample: the reference distribution is each refit's own training window (node covers of the W rows, of the bagged
  rows for the bagged models), so phi_0 is that refit's training-mean forecast and nothing depends on a background
  choice; interventional SHAP would cost a background-size factor more and make the numbers depend on the background
  chosen.  Caveat stated in advance: with correlated predictors (adjacent HAR horizons, EW / VW pairs, value and
  availability averages) the split of credit among substitutes follows the trees' split choices; the variable-level
  sum removes the within-variable split, not the split between correlated variables.
- LightGBM: booster.predict(pred_contrib=True); XGBoost: Booster.predict(DMatrix, pred_contribs=True); random forest:
  shap.TreeExplainer(model) on the sklearnex estimator: shap reads model.estimators_ (sklearnex exposes them as
  scikit-learn DecisionTreeRegressor objects built from the oneDAL forest; tree_.weighted_n_node_samples are the
  covers; shap recomputes the internal node values from them).  Checked on a toy forest before writing: accepted,
  additivity 7e-15.  If it fails on the real fits, the route used instead is documented in the README.
- A variable's SHAP at row i: phi_iA = sum_{j in A} phi_ij (all its columns: value and availability averages; HAR
  block; calendar).  A variable whose columns are all constant in a refit's window gets phi = 0 exactly there.

MEASURES (all per model: LightGBM, XGBoost, random forest)
- M1 importance shares.  I_A = mean_i |phi_iA| over the rows considered; share s_A = I_A / sum_B I_B (48 groups);
  exogenous share s^ex_A = I_A / sum_{B exogenous} I_B.  Bucket and class shares = sums of member shares.  Overall
  (all 33,970 rows, equal weight) and by calendar year of the forecast row (2014 .. 2024; 2024 = January-April).
  Secondary (column level, the analogue of the linear sum|beta| s.d.): I'_A = sum_{j in A} mean_i |phi_ij|.
  Also per variable: the number of origins at which it has a non-constant column in the window and at which f_k
  splits on it.
- M2 rank agreement with the linear ridge.  Reference: the ridge s.d.-weighted share of the W = 3,000 walk of the
  2014-start window sweep (frozen alpha 1e3) at the same 126 origins, fiw_gamma.npz walk_w3000_ridge_gamma:
  S^sd_A = sum_k sum_{j in A} |gamma_kj| / sum_k sum_{exogenous j} |gamma_kj| (gamma = coefficient on the
  window-standardized column = |beta| s.d.; checked against fiw_variables.csv pooled_share).  Primary: Spearman
  correlation of the 46 exogenous variables' tree s^ex_A (pooled over all rows) with S^sd_A, and the overlap of the two
  top-10 lists (of 10).  Secondary: the same on the 48 groups (backbone included, ridge share over all 564 columns),
  with the tree column-level I'_A, and with the lasso and elastic net (W = 3,000 walks, same file); tree-vs-tree
  Spearman.  The "more or less" question: D = NON-STANDARD share of the exogenous total, tree minus ridge (pooled
  over origins); interval from the paired moving-block bootstrap over origins (below).  Trees lean MORE on the
  non-standard variables if the 95% interval of D is above 0, LESS if below 0, else NO CLEAR DIFFERENCE.  D is also
  reported with the backbone in the denominator and with sumvolume / numobs moved to NON-STANDARD.
- M3 response shape, for each NON-STANDARD variable with s_A >= 0.5% (overall, M1) for that model; if fewer than 5
  pass, the model's 5 largest NON-STANDARD variables (labelled "below 0.5%").  x = the variable's dominant column:
  its value-average column (not availability) with the largest mean_i |phi_ij| for that model.  Bins of x over the
  33,970 rows: deciles (equal-count, pd.qcut, duplicates dropped, so a column with ties at the edges gets fewer bins);
  a column with <= 10 distinct values gets one bin per value.  m_b = mean of phi_iA in bin b (primary; the variable's
  total SHAP); also the mean of the dominant column's own phi and the 10-90% band.  Shape rule on m_1..m_B
  (B bins, R = max_b m_b - min_b m_b):
    FLAT                  R < 0.5 I_A (the bin means move less than half the variable's mean |SHAP|: no stable
                          main effect; the variable acts through dispersion within bins / interactions);
    MONOTONE (up / down)  |Spearman(b, m_b)| >= 0.9;
      THRESHOLD           a MONOTONE profile whose largest single adjacent step is >= 0.5 |m_B - m_1|;
    U-SHAPED / INVERTED-U otherwise, if the minimum (maximum) is at an interior bin b* and both ends exceed (fall
                          short of) it by >= 0.25 R;
    IRREGULAR             anything else;
    B <= 3 bins           STEP (sign and size of the steps).
  ASYMMETRIC flag on every non-FLAT profile: arms a_lo = m_1 - m_c, a_hi = m_B - m_c (m_c = mean of the middle
  bin(s)); ASYMMETRIC if the larger |arm| >= 2 x the smaller, labelled top-tail or bottom-tail.  The answer to
  "monotone, threshold, U-shaped or asymmetric?" is the shape plus the flag.  SHAP in pred_adj units and as % of the
  mean forecast over the rows.
- M4 conditional importance: s_A, bucket and class shares (of the total and of the exogenous total) on row subsets:
  (a) VIX tercile at the origin: the Cboe VIX daily close (data/free_feed/vix_1d_yahoo.parquet) on the last trading
  day before the origin date; terciles over the 126 origins (42 each); every row of N_k takes k's tercile;
  (b) bar of the day: the 13 bars (bar end 10:00 .. 16:00 ET);
  (c) FOMC statement days (scheduled: 'fomc release' > 0 in data/releases.parquet, minus src/data/loading.py
  UNSCHEDULED_FOMC_DAYS) vs all other days, by the forecast row's date.
  Contrasts with bootstrap intervals: NON-STANDARD share, high minus low VIX tercile; FOMC days minus other days;
  16:00 minus 10:00.  Also the 5 largest NON-STANDARD variables in each subset.
- M5 interactions (LightGBM, XGBoost; not the forest).  Fixed subsample: 8 rows of each N_k drawn without
  replacement by numpy default_rng(20261023), origin by origin in order (1,008 rows), each explained by its f_k.
  Phi_ijl = SHAP interaction values (XGBoost: pred_interactions; LightGBM: shap.TreeExplainer.shap_interaction_values),
  variable matrix G_iAB = sum_{j in A} sum_{l in B} Phi_ijl.
    interaction share (variable level, primary) = sum_i sum_{A != B} |G_iAB| / sum_i sum_{A,B} |G_iAB|; column level
    the same with Phi_ijl; per variable x_A = sum_i sum_{B != A} |G_iAB| / sum_i sum_B |G_iAB|, pooled by class;
    bucket x bucket (10 x 10: har_ma, calendar, 8 exogenous categories): T_PQ = mean_i |2 sum_{A in P, B in Q} G_iAB|
    for P != Q, T_PP = mean_i |sum_{A, B in P} G_iAB|, as shares of sum_{P <= Q} T_PQ;
    top 10 variable pairs (A != B, at least one NON-STANDARD) by T_AB = mean_i |2 G_iAB|, as a share of
    sum_{A <= B} T_AB.

UNCERTAINTY
- Moving-block bootstrap over the 126 origins (block 12 origins ~ one year, B = 2,000, seed 20261023, circular), the
  same resampled origins for every model and for the ridge (paired); every statistic recomputed on each resample; 95%
  percentile intervals.  This is variation over the evaluation span's neighbourhoods, not the sampling error of one
  fit.  M5 is descriptive (no intervals).
