2. ARMS (each a column subset of the final full-set design, rows and every value unchanged)
   lo_<g>   leave one group out: the full set without group g's columns (8 arms)
   add_<g>  add one group: the HAR benchmark set plus group g's columns (8 arms); add_rm is the augmented HAR "HAR +
            the futures' realized moments"
   Every step of the data preparation and of the scaling acts column by column (pool_linear_pretune.py docstring, gated
   bit for bit there), so a column subset of the full design is the design the pipeline builds for that subset of
   series; gate 0 checks it on the HAR benchmark set (the 12 columns of the full design = the baseline design).
   Classical HAR extensions (augmented-HAR benchmarks) from the same E-mini one-minute data (data/core_stats.parquet:
   sumret2 = RV, sumpret2 = RS+, sumret4, numobs, sumret).  All inputs exist; three new series are built by the
   pipeline itself (same load, domain rule, diurnal adjustment, variance-stabilizing map by name, winsorizing,
   trailing averages on the 48-interval grid, rolling scaling, late-start and unobserved rules), named so the
   existing name rules treat them as their siblings:
     sumnret2 = RS- = sumret2 - sumpret2 (>= 0 on every bar of the data; clipped at 0 against rounding), as sumpret2:
                non-negative, mean diurnal adjustment, sqrt
     rvsqrq   = RV x sqrt(RQ), RQ = (numobs / 3) x sumret4 (Barndorff-Nielsen--Shephard realized quarticity), as RV:
                non-negative, mean diurnal adjustment, sqrt -- so its k = 1 average is sqrt(RV_{t-1} RQ_{t-1}^{1/2}),
                the HARQ interaction RV x RQ^{1/2} of Bollerslev, Patton and Quaedvlieg (2016) in the design's
                volatility units (the target and the HAR terms are square roots of diurnally adjusted RV)
     negret   = min(sumret, 0), the half hour's negative return (Corsi and Reno 2012), as sumret: signed, s.d. diurnal
                adjustment, identity (mode-inflated at 0: the existing hurdle / scale-guard rules apply)
   built in one design (bucket abl_new = sumret, sumret4, sumpret2 [reference series, must equal the full design's
   columns bit for bit, gate 0] + sumnret2, rvsqrq, negret).  Arms (value averages only; the E-mini series are observed
   on every bar, so their availability averages are constant -- asserted on the full design for sumret and sumpret2):
     shar     HAR + RS+^(1) + RS-^(1)                     (Patton and Sheppard 2015: the k = 1 term split by sign)
     harq     HAR + (RV RQ^{1/2})^(1)                      (BPQ 2016 HARQ: the k = 1 interaction)
     lhar     HAR + negret^(k), k in K                      (Corsi and Reno 2012 leverage, the 6 horizons)
     sharql   HAR + the 9 columns above                    (all three extensions together)
   The original HAR is the template's baseline (OLS a0 row of Table 1).
