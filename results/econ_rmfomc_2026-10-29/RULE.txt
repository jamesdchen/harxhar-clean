0. QUESTION
   Do the 36 series beyond HAR + RM + FOMC add trading value? Within each model class, the sign strategy on the
   class's full-predictor-set forecast against the sign strategy on its HAR + RM + FOMC forecast. Secondary: what
   HAR + RM + FOMC gains over the HAR benchmark set, over the OLS-HAR strategy (the primary benchmark, D2) and over the
   unconditional short straddle, as the paper reports for the full set.

3. STRATEGY (the paper's, unchanged and not re-implemented)
   The sign strategy of Section 7: q_t = sign(plain_t - IV_t^2), ties short (np.where(plain > iv_var, 1, -1)), one
   straddle held from 15:30 ET to the close; mid return q_t R_t; bid/ask return master_table_gui.rows._crossed_return
   (a purchase pays the ask, a sale receives the bid, spread at entry only). The rows are built by the code path of
   make_rehaul_econ_main.add_ols_har (the same frame columns), so they are traded and scored as every pooled row.
   No sizing rule, no other strategy.

4. STATISTICS (make_rehaul_econ_main.row_stats and pair, unchanged; author decision D19)
   - SR: the paper's Sharpe ratio, sqrt(q) mean / sd (ddof 1) of the 614 post-warmup daily returns, q = T / sum_y c_y
     = 225.5 (hac_inference.paper_sharpe), at mid and at quoted bid/ask prices; mean daily return in % of the premium.
   - Delta SR of two strategies (a minus b) on the same 614 days, at mid and at bid/ask, with the p-value of the
     Ledoit-Wolf (2008) test of equal Sharpe ratios (me.sr_p: hac_inference.lw_sharpe_test, block 10, 4,999
     resamples, seed 20080901), the test of record.
   - Check only (CSV): the paired circular block bootstrap p of the pooled difference (me.boot_p).
   - The fixed-b t (and p) of the mean daily mid-return differential (me.nw_t / me.hac_p: Newey-West, bandwidth
     S = 33, fixed-b critical values).

5. COMPARISONS (per model class c; the tests of record at mid, bid/ask reported alongside)
   For c in {ridge, lasso, elastic net, LightGBM, XGBoost, random forest}:
     (P) PRIMARY: full set_c minus HAR + RM + FOMC_c. "The 36 series add trading value in class c" iff Delta SR > 0
         with Ledoit-Wolf p < 0.05 at mid-quotes.
     (H) HAR + RM + FOMC_c minus HAR set_c (the class's own HAR-set forecast; the three linear ones coincide with the
         OLS-HAR, Table tab:rh_econ_main note b).
     (O) HAR + RM + FOMC_c minus the OLS-HAR strategy (poolpt13_ols_baseline, the primary benchmark, D2).
     (S) HAR + RM + FOMC_c minus the unconditional short straddle (q = -1): Delta SR with its Ledoit-Wolf p, and the
         fixed-b t of the mean daily return differential.
   OLS (the OLS on HAR + RM + FOMC, the augmented-HAR benchmark): (H) = (O), against the OLS-HAR, and (S); no (P),
   since the paper has no OLS on the full predictor set.
   Reported: per row SR (mid, bid/ask), mean daily return (mid, bid/ask), every Delta SR with its p; counts over the
   six classes of Delta SR > 0 and of Delta SR > 0 with p < 0.05, for (P), (H), (O) and (S); the smallest p of (P).
   No multiplicity adjustment. Each reading is stated for the mid-quote test; nothing is selected on the results.
