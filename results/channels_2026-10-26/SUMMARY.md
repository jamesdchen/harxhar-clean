## Choices where the RULE is silent

- Inputs on a branch: an input missing from this worktree is read as a COMMITTED file of its run's branch (insurance-selling-2026-09-19-e34-ablations, insurance-selling-2026-09-19-e34-ladder) with git cat-file; the source and commit are in the provenance table.  Once the branches are merged the worktree files are read instead (same bytes).
- Linear ablation arms: every linear arm (incl. lo_<g>, g != fomc, and add_fomc) is on the patched design (linear_wide_fix, ablation addendum D), so no linear number here is pre-patch.  The trees' lo_<g>, g != fomc, are pre-patch and set against the pre-patch full set (dagger).
- 'Bars 14:30-16:00' = the bars with END stamps 14:30, 15:00, 15:30, 16:00 (the paper's bar labels); the statement (14:00) falls in the 14:30 bar.
- Gain shares (item 2) are shares of the sum over bars of (HAR loss - full loss); every day has 13 bars, so the sum of daily means is proportional to it.  Panel b of the figure is drawn as two axes (b: EVENT, c: ORDINARY) because the scales differ by an order of magnitude; panel a adds a dashed line with the EVENT days left out.
- DM on a day type: daily means over that day type's days only (81 or 2,498), HAC lag fixed at 8 for both.
- 'Leave-one-out is significant' = the full set without g has the significantly HIGHER loss (DM > 0, two-sided p < 0.05); a significant improvement from dropping g does not count (the literal two-sided variant is checked above).
- Item 3 (iii) uses the ladder's PRIMARY (pre-sample) ranking and its own k = 0 rung; R_k is not defined (-) when the full rung does not beat the k = 0 rung on that day type.
- Readings: tests in the RULE's order (CONCENTRATED, then REDUNDANT, then MIXED); a class whose reading depends on a pending arm is PENDING; one that depends on an arm not run (the forest's lo_<g> other than rm and fomc) is INCOMPLETE and left out of the model-type vote ('the forest where its arms exist'); if the joint gain is not positive the reading is NO JOINT GAIN.  Model type: the reading held by a strict majority of the eligible classes (2 of 3, or both of LightGBM and XGBoost when the forest is out); SPLIT otherwise.
- Item 1 agreement is decided before every correction is in when the two known ones already differ by more than 2 pp (then 'disagree'); a correction that is not run makes it n/a unless the other two already differ by more than 2 pp.
