RULE: the two channels of the pooled gain (FOMC event days / ordinary days) and the shared-factor test
Written 2026-10-26 by the coordinator at the author's request, committed before any scoring on the FOMC-patched
forecasts under it.

DISCLOSURE. An exploratory rescoring of the PRE-patch ablation arms by day type was seen on 26 October (coordinator
scratch, exfomc_ablate.py / fomc_decomp.py / nofomc_score.py): on ordinary days the full set beats the HAR benchmark by
2.0-4.0% (linear) and 5.3-6.1% (trees); HAR + the futures' realized moments is the largest single group; leaving out
any one group costs about nothing except the realized moments; ridge and elastic net beat the lasso on ordinary days and
the lasso wins on FOMC days; about half to three-quarters of the every-bar gain comes from the afternoons (14:30-16:00)
of the 81 statement days. The rules below fix the tests now; every number the paper reports on this question comes from
the patched forecasts under them, and the exploratory numbers are not reported.

0. COMMON
- Design of record: Table 1 (13 regular-hours bars pooled, base-5 HAR on the 48-interval grid, W = 3,000 trading days,
  FOMC-patched design D13), 2 January 2014 to 30 April 2024; scored set = Table 1's 33,527 bars.
- Day types, fixed by the Fed's published calendar, known before each forecast: EVENT = the 81 scheduled FOMC statement
  days of the span (data/releases.parquet 'fomc release', plus loading.FOMC_MISSING_STATEMENTS, minus
  loading.UNSCHEDULED_FOMC_DAYS); ORDINARY = every other scored day. No other split is used for inference; splits by bar
  of day are descriptive only.
- Loss: QLIKE on the bar's actual RV (plain = pred_adj^2 * B); MSE reported alongside. Tests: Diebold-Mariano with the
  HLN correction on the daily mean of the day's bar losses, HAC lag 8, two-sided, 5% level. Every class and every
  predictor group is reported; nothing is dropped after scoring.
- Benchmarks as Table 1: the OLS on the HAR set (D2) for ridge, lasso and elastic net; each tree's own HAR-set fit.
- An input not yet on the patched design is used only if it is design-invariant to the patch (it holds no FOMC column),
  or else labelled pre-patch in every output that uses it.

1. WHAT DO THE OTHER PREDICTORS ADD BEYOND FOMC? Three corrections, each per class:
  (a) full set vs HAR + FOMC (the add_fomc arm), every bar;
  (b) full set without FOMC (the lo_fomc arm) vs HAR, every bar;
  (c) full set vs HAR on ORDINARY days.
  Headline: (a). "The corrections agree" for a class if its three percentage changes lie within 2 percentage points of
  one another; a disagreement is reported, not resolved.

2. WHERE DOES THE GAIN COME FROM? Descriptive, no test:
  - cumulative sum through time of the daily QLIKE differential (full minus HAR), EVENT days marked; one linear and one
    tree class, chosen now as the Table 1 classes with the largest |DM|: elastic net and LightGBM;
  - mean differential by bar of day, EVENT vs ORDINARY days, every class;
  - share of each class's total gain that falls on EVENT days, and on the bars 14:30-16:00 of EVENT days.

3. CONCENTRATED OR SPREAD, BY CHANNEL. On EVENT and ORDINARY days separately:
  (i) lasso vs ridge and lasso vs elastic net, full sets (DM);
  (ii) every group g: add-alone (HAR + g vs HAR) and leave-one-out (full minus g vs full);
  (iii) the feature-count ladder, R_k = (L_0 - L_k) / (L_0 - L_46) on each day type (the patched ladder when it exists,
       else the pre-patch ladder, labelled).
  Reading, per channel and model type (linear: majority of ridge, lasso, elastic net; trees: LightGBM and XGBoost, the
  forest where its arms exist):
  - CONCENTRATED if one group's add-alone gain is at least 75% of the joint gain (full vs HAR on that day type) and its
    leave-one-out is significant;
  - REDUNDANT (correlated proxies) if the add-alone gains summed over groups exceed 1.5 times the joint gain and at most
    one group's leave-one-out is significant;
  - otherwise MIXED.

4. SHARED FACTOR OR DENSE? Factor-augmented HAR (diffusion index, Stock and Watson 2002), linear, OLS, no penalty:
  - Factor block: the value averages (every horizon) of every exogenous series except the FOMC group (the event
    channel); no availability averages. Standardized within each estimation window. Principal components of the
    window's correlation matrix; loadings recomputed once a trading day from the window ending at the previous bar.
  - Forecast: OLS on [HAR set, first k component scores], re-estimated at every bar on Table 1's 39,000-row window,
    k in {1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 20}. Supervised variant: partial least squares components of the same block against the
    target, same schedule, k in {1, 2, 3}.
  - Gate, before any scoring: k = 0 reproduces Table 1's OLS-HAR forecasts to 1e-10 relative on every bar.
  - Primary scoring on ORDINARY days, also every bar and EVENT days. Recovery R_k = (L_HAR - L_k) / (L_HAR - L_full),
    L_full = the lower of Table 1's full-set ridge and elastic net QLIKE on the same subset.
  - Reading (ORDINARY days): SHARED FACTOR if some k <= 3 (PCA or PLS) has R_k >= 0.75 and its loss is not
    significantly above the full set's; DENSE BEYOND A FEW FACTORS if R_10 < 0.5 and the full set beats HAR + 10
    components significantly; otherwise INTERMEDIATE.
  - Descriptive: mean share of the block's variance in components 1-3 across windows; the first component's loadings
    summed by predictor group.
  - Recorded now: Table 1's full sets are penalized and include availability averages; HAR + k components is OLS on
    value averages only. The comparison is of forecast accuracy, not a nesting.
