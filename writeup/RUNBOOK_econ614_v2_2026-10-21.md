## What changed

- **N15c** (Section 7.3, commit "Economics 7.3 (note N15c) ..."): three sentences print counts and ranges instead of
  "all" and a named top list; make_rehaul_results.py writes `\rhrPoolShXHarMin/Max`, `\rhrPoolShXFullMin/Max`.
- **Decision #2, the lag rule.** The Newey-West lag floor(1.5 T^(1/3)) (`\fqsHACLag` = 14, `\rhrHACLag` =
  `\rheHACLag` = 12) is gone from every test and from the text. New shared module `writeup/hac_inference.py`:
  - tests of a **mean** (mean return of a strategy, mean daily return differential): Lazarus, Lewis, Stock and Watson
    (2018) -- Newey-West (Bartlett) with bandwidth S = ceil(1.3 T^(1/2)) = 33 at T = 614, divisor T, fixed-b p-values
    and critical value (Kiefer and Vogelsang 2005) from a simulated limit (1,000,000 draws, fixed seed; about one
    minute, once a process; simulated 5 % critical value 2.105, Kiefer-Vogelsang cubic 2.121);
  - tests of a **Sharpe-ratio difference**: Ledoit and Wolf (2008) studentized circular block bootstrap, block 10
    (sensitivity 5 and 20), 4,999 resamples, fixed seed.
  Every test runs on the 614 post-warmup days (t > WARMUP = 252), including make_fig_qlike_sharpe.py (was 866 days,
  lag 14). Diebold-Mariano tests are unchanged.
- Significance counts now use the fixed-b p < 0.05 (mean claims: `\rhrPoolShortSig`, `\rheSigVsShort`,
  `\rheSigVsShortFull`, `\rhrAfPoolDiffSig`, `\rhrPoolVsCloseRetSig`) or the Ledoit-Wolf p < 0.05 (Sharpe claims:
  `\rhePairsSig`, new `\rhePairsSigNeg`, `\rheAfEnetLassoSig`, `\rhrAfPoolShHigherSig`, `\fqsShHigherSig`,
  `\fqsShLowerSig`, and the stars on every Delta SR in the parked tables E1-E3, T1, T2).
- New appendix table `generated/rehaul_app_hac_robust.tex` (34 rows: pooled vs short straddle 12; pooled full vs HAR 6;
  pooled vs closing-interval 12; closing-interval elastic net and lasso full vs HAR 2; closing-interval elastic net vs
  short 1; short straddle mean vs 0 1), written by make_rehaul_results.py and `\input` by
  rehaul/10_statistical_tests.tex, with a paragraph and the counts `\rhrRob...`.
- Text: 07_economics.tex (7.2 measures, 7.3 significance sentences, N15c, note E16 parked), 10_statistical_tests.tex
  (the test of a mean, the test of equal Sharpe ratios, the robustness item, the reporting convention), 10_appendix.tex
  (Appendix F sentences, overview items, reproducibility list). references.bib: Andrews1991, NeweyWest1994,
  KieferVogelsang2005, LazarusLewisStockWatson2018 (verified against Crossref).
