\section{Master Table of Campaign Arms}
\label{sec:appendix_master}

frozen panel (Section~\ref{sec:data_prep}), one scoring contract
(Section~\ref{sec:smearing}), every arm scored on the identical rows.

is the Diebold--Mariano $t$-statistic of the per-bar loss differential
against the benchmark. Product- and transmission-block arms with a
$24{,}000$-bar window are scored from the first row outside their frozen
selection and frame windows.
