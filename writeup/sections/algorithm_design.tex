\section{Algorithm Design}
\label{sec:algorithms}

With the feature set frozen, the estimation \emph{algorithm} becomes the design
space. This section presents the measurement instrument the rest of the paper
depends on---a causal hyperparameter-selection protocol---together with the
exact every-bar estimators that make it computable, and one proposed
nonparametric estimator whose ablations are decisive against its novel
component.

\subsection{Causal Hyperparameter Selection}
\label{sec:causal_tuning}

Section~\ref{sec:tuning_leak} showed what happens when hyperparameters are
selected on the evaluation panel. The protocol below is the replacement, and
every arm in Sections~\ref{sec:marginal} and~\ref{sec:linvsnonlin} is estimated
under it.

Every $250$ solves, each arm re-selects its own hyperparameters from a
leakage-clean forward split of its \emph{current} training window
($500$ trading days, $24{,}000$ bars):

\begin{enumerate}
  \item a fit block, taking all but the last $150$ bars of the window;
  \item a $25$-bar embargo, chosen to cover the longest moving average
    entering the features, so that no validation row shares a rolling window
    with a fit row;
  \item a $125$-bar validation tail, on which configurations are scored by
    mean squared error.
\end{enumerate}

The winning configuration is then used for the next $250$ forecasts and
discarded. No forecast's hyperparameters are ever selected using information
from that forecast's own future, and no forecast's hyperparameters are selected
on QLIKE, so the evaluation loss is never the selection loss.

Linear arms (ridge, recursive lasso, recursive elastic net) search log-scaled
penalty grids and slide warm between tunings via the exact updates of
Section~\ref{sec:exact_estimators}, cold-reseeding at each re-selection. Tree
arms search max-depth $\{3,5,8\} \times$ colsample $\{0.25, 0.5\}$ with the
learning rate pinned at the production value, fit grid cells at 300 trees and
refit the winner every bar at 500. Model refit is every bar for all arms, so
the refit-cadence asymmetry that confounded earlier comparisons is absent.

We regard this protocol, rather than any individual arm, as the section's
primary contribution: it converts ``tuning does not help here'' from an
anecdote about one search into a claim with a defensible causal structure.

\subsection{Exact Every-Bar Estimators}
\label{sec:exact_estimators}

Per-bar refitting over $218{,}934$ bars, crossed with 45 arms and a periodic
hyperparameter search, is only feasible if the updates are exact and
incremental.

\paragraph{Rank-1 rolling least squares.} Adding and dropping one observation
from a rolling-window least-squares fit are rank-1 modifications of the
Gram matrix, handled by the Sherman--Morrison identity. This makes the
every-bar OLS incumbent exact and computable at panel scale, and it is what
allows the benchmark to be a genuinely per-bar refit rather than a cadence
approximation.

\paragraph{Online elastic-net homotopy.} The $\ell_1$ analogue of the same
idea. As the window slides, the elastic-net solution path is updated by
homotopy continuation \citep{Garrigues2008}: the active set and the signs of
the active coefficients change at a finite number of breakpoints, between
which the solution is piecewise linear in the perturbation. This delivers the
exact recursive lasso and elastic-net arms rather than warm-started
approximations, so that the ``selection loses to shrinkage'' finding of
Section~\ref{sec:tuning_nonlever} is not confounded with optimisation error.

\paragraph{Block ridge.} Where the design partitions naturally---backbone
versus exogenous block---separate penalties per block are solved jointly,
which is what allows the backbone to be lightly penalised while the wide
exogenous block is shrunk hard.

\subsection{Spectral \texorpdfstring{$k$}{k}-Nearest Neighbours}
\label{sec:spectral_knn}

The proposed estimator asks whether the dense-but-weak regime rewards analog
retrieval: embed trailing residual trajectories with Laplacian eigenmaps
\citep{BelkinNiyogi2003}, retrieve past episodes that are near in the embedded
geometry, and forecast by locally averaging their outcomes. The design has four
axes: the residualiser (OLS-anchored or identity), the trajectory window $W$,
the embedding dimension $d$ (with $d = 0$ denoting passthrough, i.e.\ no
embedding), and the neighbourhood size $k$.

The manifold-embedding step---the novel component of the design---hurts on all
eight matched configurations, by between $0.00145$ and $0.02397$, and the
damage is an order of magnitude larger without the OLS anchor than with it.
The measured cause is that the out-of-sample Nystr\"om extension error is of
the same order as the embedding scale itself: new points cannot be placed on
the learned manifold accurately enough for retrieval in the embedded space to
beat retrieval in the ambient space.

\subsubsection{What is load-bearing is not novel}

The same table identifies what does the work.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{The OLS anchor is load-bearing}: at $d = 0$, replacing the
    identity residualiser with the OLS anchor is worth between $0.00768$ and
    $0.01321$ across matched cells---five to nine times the size of any
    model-class effect in this paper.
  \item \textbf{Large-neighbourhood averaging is load-bearing}: moving from
    $k = 25$ to $k = 100$ is worth $\approx 0.0018$ in the anchored,
    unembedded cells. Heavy local averaging, not sharp local structure, is what
    the regime rewards---which is the nonparametric restatement of
    dense-but-weak.
  \item \textbf{The embedding is not}, as above.
\end{itemize}

The surviving estimator is therefore an OLS-anchored, unembedded, heavily
averaged analog search. It is a reasonable method and it is not novel. We
report this as the section's finding rather than burying it: in the
dense-but-weak regime nonparametric flexibility is a cost to be justified, not
a default.

\subsubsection{The headline cell is a selected minimum}
\label{sec:knn_selection}

The best kNN arm reaches QLIKE $0.13096$ against the incumbent's $0.13415$, at
DM $t = -17.6$. Three qualifications belong with that number, and earlier
drafts of this paper carried none of them.

\paragraph{It is the minimum of 32 cells.} The kNN family contributes 32 arms
to the battery, of which 8 beat the incumbent. Quoting the minimum with an
unadjusted DM statistic, on the same panel used to select it, is a
winner's-curse figure. The honest presentations are either a pre-registered
cell or a confirmation on held-out data.
\pending{holdout confirmation of the winning kNN cell, or a pre-registration
statement identifying it as chosen ex ante}

\paragraph{Its bucket is the one that survived.} The winning cell sits on the
\texttt{market\_vw} bucket with an exogenous mask; sibling buckets failed as
singular under the same configuration. Until that failure is diagnosed, the
bucket ranking within the kNN family carries no weight, and the choice of
\texttt{market\_vw} cannot be read as an information finding.

\paragraph{It does not trail every parametric arm.} At $0.13096$ the winning
kNN cell beats \textbf{25 of the 45} causally tuned parametric arms, including
the baseline LightGBM ($0.13118$), baseline XGBoost ($0.13163$), and all three
baseline linear arms ($0.13445$, $0.13471$, $0.13475$). What is true is that it
trails the best arms on the informative buckets---\texttt{all\_features},
\texttt{moments} and \texttt{liquidity}---which is the weaker and more precise
statement. An earlier draft asserted the stronger claim; it is false against
our own battery.

\subsection{Summary}
\label{sec:algo_summary}

With features frozen, exactness and anchoring matter more than expressiveness.
The causal-tuning protocol makes the paper's central negative result about
hyperparameters defensible; the rank-1 and homotopy updates make an every-bar
benchmark computable at panel scale; and the analog-retrieval experiment
resolves into a clean decomposition in which the anchor and the averaging pay
and the manifold does not. The novel component of the proposed estimator is
the component that fails, and we report it at the same altitude as the parts
that work.
