\subsection{The Shift (Dating) Test across the Model Set}
\label{sec:app_shift_test_headline}

\paragraph{What is tested.} The last-30-min trade reads each forecast's row labelled 16:00 ---
the forecast of the 15:30--16:00 bar, issued at 15:30. The test re-scores the
$\mathrm{sign}(s)$ rule with the forecast \emph{row} shifted by $k$ half-hour bars and
everything else unchanged, every shifted row placed on the traded bar's scale as
$\hat y_k^{2}B_{16{:}00}$, so that only what the model had seen changes with $k$. A forecast

% the \shiftNShiftedPooled{} pooled twins. The other \shiftNNotShifted{} ---
% \shiftNNotShiftedPerbarLinear{} per-bar linear forecasts and \shiftNNotShiftedTrees{} per-bar
% trees --- are fitted on the 16:00 bar alone, carry only that row and cannot be shifted, so
% the test does not speak for them.
can be shifted only if its table carries rows for the whole session, and of the \shiftNSet{}
forecasts of the model set only the \shiftNShiftedPaper{} paper's forecasts do. The other
\shiftNNotShifted{} carry only the 16:00 row and cannot be shifted: the
\shiftNNotShiftedPerbarLinear{} per-bar linear forecasts and \shiftNNotShiftedTrees{} per-bar
trees, fitted on the 16:00 bar alone, and the \shiftNNotShiftedPooled{} pre-tuned pooled

Scorer, trade and fills are those of Table~\ref{tab:close_option_rules}, on the
\shiftNDays{} days on which every shifted row exists.

\paragraph{Reading.} Table~\ref{tab:app_shift_test_headline} gives every $k$. At $k=0$ the
\shiftNShifted{} forecasts trade at a median Sharpe ratio of \shiftZeroMedMid{}
(\shiftZeroMinMid{} to \shiftZeroMaxMid; \shiftZeroMedCrossed{} crossed). One bar stale the
median falls to \shiftMinusOneMedMid{} (median change \shiftMinusOneMedDelta), \shiftMinusOneNBelow{}
of the \shiftNShifted{} fall below their own $k=0$ value, and the median position agrees with
the trade's on \shiftMinusOneMedAgree\,\% of days. Over the twelve stale rows between
\shiftStaleNBelowMin{} and \shiftStaleNBelowMax{} forecasts fall below $k=0$, all of them at
\shiftStaleNAllBelow{} rows; the best stale row by median is $k=\shiftBestStaleK$ at
\shiftBestStaleMedMid. The next session's rows score medians of \shiftNextMedMin{} to
\shiftNextMedMax{}, with between \shiftNextNBelowMin{} and \shiftNextNBelowMax{} forecasts below
$k=0$ (all of them at \shiftNextNAllBelow{} rows); at $k=+1$ the median is \shiftPlusOneMedMid{}
(\shiftPlusOneMedCrossed{} crossed), \shiftPlusOneNBelow{} below $k=0$, at a median agreement of
\shiftPlusOneMedAgree\,\%. The realized variance itself, in place of the forecast, scores
\shiftStarMid{} mid and \shiftStarCrossed{} crossed, against always short at
\shiftAlwaysShortMid{} (\shiftAlwaysShortCrossed{} crossed) on the same days.
