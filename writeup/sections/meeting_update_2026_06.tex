\section{Meeting Update (June 2026): Progress Against the Research Plan}
\label{sec:meeting_update_2026_06}

This update maps the work completed since the April meeting onto the research
plan discussed on 6/10 and 6/15, and---as requested---states the project's
\emph{main and novel contributions} and where they sit in the literature. Items
are bucketed \textbf{Completed (NOW)} and \textbf{In flight / Next}; a longer
research backlog (Later) is maintained separately. All QLIKE figures are
full-out-of-sample, raw-variance-scale, Duan-smeared.

\subsection{Main and novel contributions}
\label{sec:contributions_june}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{A clock-anchored intraday regime inside multi-scale HAR}
(refines Corsi, 2009). At 30-minute resolution we find HAR volatility-persistence
\emph{sign-flips at the session edges}: $\mathrm{corr}(\mathrm{har\_ma\_5},
\text{residual})$ is $+0.05\!\ldots\!+0.13$ mid-session but $-0.085$ at the open
and $-0.21$ at the close/after-hours (h17), sharp rather than gradual, and
\emph{clock-anchored} (a state/vol-regime partition fails OOS, $-0.0027$ vs the
clock partition's $+0.0095$). The mechanism is auction / session-transition
microstructure (MOC/MOO liquidity + dealer hedging), with dealer-gamma a
secondary ($\sim$20\%) modulator. A single legible linear term,
$\mathrm{HAR}\times\text{late-day}$, recovers $58\%$ of the boosted tree's edge,
making the mechanism closed-form.

\item \textbf{An additive-plus-pairwise saturation law for dense--weak signals}
(operationalises Xiu et al.\ on weak/dense factors). A functional-ANOVA of the
depth-8 tree decomposes as $\approx 55\%$ additive (HAR-dominated), $9\%$
pairwise, and $36\%$ higher-order-and-diffuse; HAR alone is $96\%$ of the
explainable variance. We confirm the signal is \emph{dense}, not low-rank:
elastic-net keeps $\sim$120 effective dimensions of 529, and PCA/PLS truncation
\emph{fails} (K5 keeps $72\%$ variance but loses all signal). This yields a
\textbf{tree-subsumption law}: a boosted tree is invariant to monotone
re-encodings of the current row, so only history/sequence functionals can beat
it---and when the only survivor is 5th-decimal, the lever is \emph{information},
not features.

\item \textbf{Endogenous regime discovery + a differentiable per-regime
mixture-of-experts} (vs.\ Graph-HAR / structured-nonparametric HAR, and
MoE-for-order-execution). Rather than imposing a graph a priori, we \emph{discover}
the regime via a reusable loop (EBM-interpret $\to$ OOS interaction test $\to$
sharp localisation $\to$ distillation $\to$ falsification gauntlet). A
differentiable MoE (soft-tree / learned-metric-attention gate over additive-NAM,
low-rank-FM, and FiLM experts) confirms the hand-found hour partition is optimal
for the close window. A precise \emph{negative} result---the ``starvation'' law---%
explains why routing on the tree residual cannot help: $r_2 = r_1 - d_8(X)$ is
orthogonal to $d_8$'s depth-8 partition of the \emph{same} features, so the gate
is both starved and subordinate (MoE-as-sole-regime $0.12494$ vs $d_8$ $0.12255$).

\item \textbf{An OOS-robust, placebo-controlled evaluation harness}
(\texttt{src/evaluation/feature\_cv.py}). Purged, embargoed walk-forward CV +
bagging + a \emph{circular-shift} permutation null (which preserves a feature's
autocorrelation while destroying target alignment---the correct null for slow
time series, where an i.i.d.\ shuffle would whiten the feature) + a three-part
significance gate (CI excludes 0 $\wedge$ replicates across folds $\wedge$ beats
the placebo). This turns Xiu's ``dense-but-weak'' regime into a rigorous go/no-go
and \emph{caught roughly six near-false-positives}---features that replicated with
a negative point estimate (entity embeddings, analog retrieval, HAR-difference and
spike aggregators) but failed the placebo. It is distinct from Diebold--Mariano /
Model-Confidence-Set (feature-level, autocorrelation-aware); it also supports
per-step, context-aware hyperparameter tuning by the same null.

\item \textbf{A price-space exhaustion result and a mechanism-ranked
data-to-buy.} Vol memory saturates at HAR scale $\approx 3125$ ($\sim$13 trading
days); longer rolling averages reach noise. The MA-aggregator space is exhausted
(geometric is 70--90\% \emph{spanned} by rolling-HAR; EWMA/RMS/median are either
already-in-the-base or null), and the entire spike/extreme axis ($\sim$12 forms:
max, quantiles, drawdown, vol-of-vol, clustering) is \emph{orthogonal to
rolling-HAR yet signal-less}, as a main effect \emph{and} as interactions
(spike$\times$level, spike$\times$time both null)---so \textbf{vol is forecastable
through its level, not its extremes}, and \emph{persistence is regime-independent
of spikiness}. The remaining lever is finer-granularity microstructure, ranked by
mechanism in \S\ref{sec:next_june}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Next (in flight / validated-marginal)}
\label{sec:next_june}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Acquire and gate the data-to-buy} (the only lever not at the floor),
ranked by mechanism: (1) \emph{auction imbalance} (NOII / MOC--MOO), the direct
cause of the session-edge sign-flip; (2) \emph{order-flow imbalance / signed
depth} (an aggregate turnover-OFI proxy is null; a per-name signed version is
new); (3) \emph{dealer gamma exposure} (GEX), the $\sim$20\% modulator;
(4) richer per-name, higher-frequency \emph{sentiment/attention}. Each enters
through the harness (sample $\to$ feature $\to$ gate) and must clear the
circular-shift placebo before full acquisition. A mechanism-targeted feature is
$\sim$27$\times$ more valuable per feature than a raw exogenous column
(\texttt{cumrv} $-0.00122$ vs 359 exog $-0.00449$), which is why finer
microstructure---not more aggregate regressors---is the ask.
\item \textbf{Materialise the missing external sources}: NASDAQ Open--Close and
macro/corporate-event (OPEX, index-rebalance) calendars; build the signed
per-name OFI from the buy/sell turnover already loaded.
\item \textbf{Model-level significance}: add Diebold--Mariano and a Model
Confidence Set over the model families, complementing the feature-level gate.
\item \textbf{Clustering / per-cluster models}: infrastructure and ablation CSVs
exist; document the global-vs-per-cluster trade-off (the natural extension of the
temporal-kNN$\to$attention framing).
\end{itemize}
