\subsection{The Model Set Compounded at a Fixed Fraction of Wealth}
\label{sec:app_compounding_headline}

\paragraph{What is computed.} The fixed-fraction exercise of
Section~\ref{sec:close_option_methods} --- a share $f=\cmpF$ of current wealth deployed as
straddle premium every day, the same on every day and for every rule, wealth
$W_T=\prod_t(1+f\,q_tR_t)$, the annualized mean of $\log(1+fR'_t)$ as the growth rate, every
wealth factor asserted positive --- run for the $\mathrm{sign}(s)$ positions of each of the
\cmpNSet{} forecasts of the model set, on the 866 days and at both fills, beside always short
(Table~\ref{tab:app_compounding_headline}, Figure~\ref{fig:app_compounding_headline}). The
crossed frame pays the ask and hits the bid on every day.

\paragraph{Reading.} At the midpoint \cmpNPosMid{} of the \cmpNSet{} forecasts end above one
unit of wealth (median terminal wealth \cmpWMedMid, from \cmpWLoMid{} to \cmpWHiMid; median
annualized log-growth \cmpGMedMid, from \cmpGLoMid{} to \cmpGHiMid), and \cmpNPosX{} across the
spread (median \cmpWMedX, from \cmpWLoX{} to \cmpWHiX; median log-growth \cmpGMedX). Always
short ends at \cmpAW{} (\cmpAG) at the midpoint and \cmpAWX{} across the spread. The median
fall of wealth from its running peak is \cmpDDMedMid{} percent at the midpoint and
\cmpDDMedX{} across the spread (largest \cmpDDMaxMid{} and \cmpDDMaxX), and the worst single
day removes up to \cmpWorstDayMax{} percent of a forecast's wealth. The ruin bound
$1/|\min_t R'_t|$ is at least \cmpRuinMin{} over the model set (always short \cmpARuin), and
$f=\cmpF$ sits below it for \cmpNRuinOK{} of the \cmpNSet{} forecasts. By calendar year,
annualized from the days traded in each, the number of forecasts with a positive midpoint
log-growth is \cmpYearsNPosMid{} (medians \cmpYearsMedMid) and across the spread
\cmpYearsNPosX{} (medians \cmpYearsMedX).
