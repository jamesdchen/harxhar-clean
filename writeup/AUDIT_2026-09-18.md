1. Every "improvement" in proposals 30-38 was scored on an uncosted, unlagged book. Proposal 27 showed the crossed
   exit book nets Sharpe 1.20 at 0.5 bp hedge cost (era), about 0 at 1 bp or with a one-bar lag. The two surviving

2. Session-wide multiple testing. About 830 inferential cells in one night. On the rule-adoption gates (30, 31, 33,
   35B, 37) 576 cells produced 18 passes where 28.8 are expected under the null: fewer adopted rules than chance. No
   rule pass survives Holm within its own study. Two results survive correction: the combined next-bar forecaster (35A)
   and the bias-corrected delta V9 (36; survives Bonferroni across all 24 hedge-volatility variants).
3. The hedge-density mechanism is not established. Proposal 39's "denser hedging pays more gamma" rests on the sum of
   squared one-minute returns (index panel) exceeding the squared 30-minute move (vendor spot tape) by 5-16 percent:
   two price sources, so a microstructure or stale-print mismatch is as likely as within-bar mean reversion. The
   primary-book bootstrap already said "no evidence". Retract "30 minutes is near-optimal because of mean reversion";
   keep "no evidence finer hedging helps, and turnover cost dominates".
4. Proposal 37 was settled by algebra. f_comb minus slice equals w times (rv_hat minus slice) with w positive on every
   day, so the combined sign rule is the ridge rule; the same identity made 35B's zero-margin cells redundant. Its
   passes (XGBoost, LightGBM sign rules) are proposal 30's forecast zoo re-screened; a different forecast wins each study
   (a0 in 30, xgb and lgbm in 37, blk2 nowhere), the signature of noise selection.

   settlement), and that is the expected path because the crossing cap ($0.10) is below the fixture's spread and the
   option tick is wrong above $3 (0.10, not 0.05); partial fills are lost and mis-signed; the kill switch marks futures
   at the index; a non-invertible vol silently unwinds the whole hedge; `--mode paper --port 7496` reaches live; four
   DaySummary defects; the restart guard swallows exceptions (double-entry risk); no snapshot timeout. The 58 green
   tests cannot see any of it. README's "ES at four straddles equals MES at one" is false (parity needs n=10).
