\subsection*{Scorer, days, reference}
$\bullet$ \textbf{Scorer: the plain back-transform.} Every forecast is used as f\textsuperscript{2}\textperiodcentered{}B (f = the model's forecast on the adjusted scale sqrt(RV/\allowbreak{}B), B = the diurnal baseline; the \texttt{yhat}\textsuperscript{2} \textperiodcentered{} \texttt{baseline} of \texttt{results/\allowbreak{}spxw\_pnl/\allowbreak{}yhat\_*.parquet}); QLIKE (\texttt{qlike\_raw}) against the per-bar spec's 16:00 target; the trade is the deck's 15:30 sign(s) on the straddle (\texttt{trade\_1530}): buy when f\textsuperscript{2}\textperiodcentered{}B exceeds the 15:30 implied variance, sell otherwise. The research scorer's 16:00-bar recalibration (f\textsuperscript{2} + s)\textperiodcentered{}B (s = the forecast's own trailing 250-session mean squared adjusted-scale error, lagged one) was removed on 2026-10-06 (user decision); it survives only as the diagnostic column \texttt{pred\_clock} and in \texttt{recal\_vs\_plain.csv} (section below). The notebook's 13-bar Mincer--Zarnowitz map is \textbf{not} used either: no number here may be set beside a notebook number.\par
$\bullet$ \textbf{Point estimates only.} The circular block bootstrap is disabled (commit b761b28: every draw is the original sample), so no interval is reported. The inference shown is the Diebold--Mariano statistic on the daily QLIKE difference (\texttt{dm\_test}, Newey--West variance with the Harvey--Leybourne--Newbold correction) and the Newey--West HAC t of the daily mid-return difference (\texttt{newey\_west\_t}); counts 'significant' use the two-sided 5 \% level of those statistics.\par
$\bullet$ \textbf{Days:} table A = 295 forecasts in 20 families (+ 9 check rows) on the same 866 trade days (2020-01-03 .. 2024-04-30); intersection over table A = 866 of 866 trade days. Every family that covers the 866 trade days is in table A. Table B = 21 forecasts scored on their own days (paired with the reference on those days): 21 chain-period buckets forecasts from 2022-01-03 on 550 trade days. Forecasts that failed to load: none.\par
$\bullet$ \textbf{Which forecasts drop days:} no table-A forecast misses a trade day; 0 forecasts miss trade days inside their own window. The 21 table-B forecasts start at their comparison window (2022-01-03), so each omits the 316 earlier trade days by design (their implied-vol inputs are real chain data only from then; compare\_mfiv\_harlag.CHAIN\_START). Coverage per forecast: \texttt{master\_table\_days.csv}.\par
$\bullet$ \textbf{Reference:} the block-diagonal ridge (\texttt{blk2}, \texttt{yhat\_blk2\_fomc1.parquet}) --- the paper's headline forecast (HAR ladder, penalty 1, plus the exogenous block, penalty 100, on the panel of record), the forecast the paper's 15:30 deck trades. Under this scorer it has QLIKE 0.1068 and sign(s) Sharpe 1.28 mid /\allowbreak{} 0.80 crossed (the deck's own map gives it 1.34 mid; the difference is the scorer). Second reference: always short, Sharpe 0.20 mid /\allowbreak{} -0.27 crossed.\par
