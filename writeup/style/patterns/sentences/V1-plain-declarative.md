# V1. Plain declarative sentences

**Family:** sentences. **Stage:** drafting (S0), before lock. **Basis:** T-genre (Blattman's voice file;
Goldsmith-Pinkham's style guide); I (A2).

## Intent

Draft each sentence as one plain claim with its subject and verb early, so the author reads and corrects content, not
style.

## Also known as

Draft plain (S0); the simple version (A2).

## Motivation

A2: "the paper needs to start from a simple unedited state and, through my reading and correction of the simple
version, end in a finished edited state." The author reads each new layer once (§8.1). Polish written before that
reading is spent on content he may change, and it puts style between him and the claim. S0 is the drafting rule that
produces the simple version.

## Applicability

Every sentence an agent drafts for L0–L3: beats, bullets, body sentences, captions, table notes, generator note
strings. It is a drafting rule, so it applies before lock (S0). It governs new text only: it is no reason to rewrite
sentences an edit does not target (V14), and it never applies to author-supplied text (V13) or to text pulled from
Overleaf (V15).

## Structure / Rewrite

- One claim a sentence. A second claim in a subordinate clause becomes its own sentence.
- Subject and main verb early; scope, conditions and tests after them.
- No setup for a reveal, no importance assertion, no rhetorical figure. Write nothing the later tiers would remove
  (S0): the hard-flag words of AP-V and the failures of Rule NT (V5).

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| "What emerges from the ablations is a picture of redundancy among the other groups." | "Removing any one of the other groups changes the gradient-boosted trees' ordinary-day loss by at most `\rhchOrdLoOtherTreeMaxAbs`\%." |
| "Strikingly, it is the FOMC calendar that carries the gain on statement days, while on ordinary days it is the realized moments." | "The FOMC calendar carries the gain on statement days. The realized moments carry it on ordinary days." |

## Consequences

Costs: the simple version reads flat, and a split sentence may repeat its subject. Buys: the author corrects content in
one reading, and fewer flags reach the edit stage (S0's stated output).

## Detection

No automatic check: S0 is a drafting rule. What it misses shows up later as S2 and S3 markers (AP-V, AP-S), S4a cadence
flags and S4b Rule NT hits. A reviewer reads the first words of each sentence for its subject and verb and counts the
claims it makes.

## Related patterns
