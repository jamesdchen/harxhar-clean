

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:rh_conclusion}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Exogenous predictors in intraday realized variance forecasts.} We ask what exogenous predictors, from
  the futures' own realized moments and the constituents' trading to the FOMC calendar, implied volatility and
  alternative data, add to pooled forecasts of the E-mini S\&P~500's realized variance on every regular-hours bar,
  against OLS on the HAR benchmark set.
\item \textbf{The exogenous predictors improve the forecast of every bar} (Section~\ref{sec:rh_results}).
  \begin{itemize}
  \item The full set lowers the average QLIKE loss in all \rhorAfPairs{} model classes, each reduction significant at
    the 5\% level, by \rhorVsOlsRedLinRng{}\% (penalized linear models) and \rhorVsOlsRedTreeRng{}\%
    (tree ensembles) against the OLS benchmark.
  \item The gain survives the HAR benchmark augmented with the futures' realized moments (significantly in
    \rhabNClassesBeatAddRm{} of the \rhabNClassesAddRm{} classes), the classical HAR extensions and a window of
    \rhrbWinLong{} days (\rhrbNFullLowerSigBarLong{} of \rhrbNModelsLong{} classes).
  \end{itemize}
\item \textbf{The gain is concentrated} (Section~\ref{sec:rh_fi}). The FOMC calendar
  carries most of the trees' gain and the futures' realized moments come second; the \ladderTopK{}
  highest-ranked series retain \ladderTreeRetainRng{}\% of it, and the linear models do best with
  \ladderLinKstarRng{} series. The signal sits in a few series rather than spreading thinly over many.
\item \textbf{Alternative data add little, even when live.}
  \begin{itemize}
  \item Under our causal construction the StockTwits and volatility-demand series are live on
    \rhlvStSentEvLo--\rhlvStAttEvHi\% and \rhlvVdEvLo--\rhlvVdEvHi\% of the scored bars; on those bars they change the
    loss little, and volatility demand raises that of the linear models.
  \item Their share of the trees' attribution rises in their live years (StockTwits
    \rhfilStwLiveMin--\rhfilStwLiveMax\%, from at most \rhfilStwWinPct\% of the training window), but use is not
    accuracy.
  \end{itemize}
\item \textbf{QLIKE and squared error.} Under squared-error loss the full set is not more accurate (its mean squared
  error is higher in \rhcaNMseHigherBarSpan{} of \rhorAfPairs{} classes, significantly in \rhcaNMseHigherSigBarSpan),
  a difference carried almost entirely by \rhcaTopDays{} days of the COVID-19 crash; without the crash window it is
  significantly lower in \rhcaNMseLowerSigBarOosExCrash{} classes.
\item \textbf{An application to 0DTE straddles} (Section~\ref{sec:rh_econ}).
  \begin{itemize}
  \item Signed by the forecast of the closing bar's variance against implied variance, one at-the-money SPXW
    straddle a day earns annualized Sharpe ratios of \rhrPoolShFullMin--\rhrPoolShFullMax{} at mid-quotes
    (\rhrPoolShXFullMin--\rhrPoolShXFullMax{} at bid/ask) on the full set, against \rheShortSh{} for the short
    straddle, significantly more in \rhemFullVsShortSig{} of the \rhorAfPairs{} classes; against the HAR strategy, in
    \rhemFullVsOlsHarSig{}.
  \item The gain is earned on the days the strategies buy and rests on their \rhedNTop{} best days; no strategy has a
    significant market beta.
  \end{itemize}
\item \textbf{Comparison with machine-learning option portfolios.}
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \citet{MaischZhangUlrichZimmer2026} trade decile long--short portfolios of S\&P~500 index options of all
    maturities, re-formed every half hour on forecasts of delta-hedged returns, over \extMaischTestMonths{} months of
    \extMaischYear. Their Sharpe ratios (\extMaischSrGbrtAnn{} and \extMaischSrEnsAnn{} annualized) are gross, at
    mid-quotes; by their account quoted spreads would erase much of the profit.
  \item Ours come from one unhedged straddle a day, are far smaller, are reported at bid/ask too, and rest on a few
    days.
  \end{itemize}
\item \textbf{Scope and extensions.} One index and one futures contract; a window fixed after an out-of-sample
  comparison of windows, all reported in the online appendix; NYSE-holiday sessions kept in the estimation sample; a
  short option sample (\rheSzDays{} days). Extensions: the sessions after \rhorSpanEnd, other markets, density
  forecasts, and trades entered at earlier bars from the every-bar forecasts already produced.
\end{itemize}
