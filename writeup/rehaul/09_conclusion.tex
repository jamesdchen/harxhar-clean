\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:rh_conclusion}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Exogenous predictors reduce QLIKE loss in pooled intraday models.} Relative to the HAR benchmark, augmenting the model with the full exogenous predictor set lowers mean QLIKE across all \rhrAfPoolPairs{} pooled intraday model classes, with \rhrAfPoolLowerSig{} of these reductions statistically significant at the 5\% level (Section~\ref{sec:rh_results}). The largest relative reductions occur in the tree ensembles, while among the penalized linear specifications the elastic net achieves a statistically significant reduction.

\item \textbf{Scope and limitations.}
  \begin{itemize}
  % 2026-10-20 (user): parked -- Table 1 scored from 2012 (TABLE1_SWITCH.md, approved). Was: "\item Our out-of-sample evaluation spans $T_{\mathrm{oos}} = \rhoosDays$ trading days from \rhoosSpanStart{} to \rhoosSpanEnd."
  \item Our out-of-sample evaluation spans $T_{\mathrm{oos}} = \rhoosDays$ trading days from \rhoosSpanStart{} to \rhoosSpanEnd; the forecast accuracy of the pooled linear models (Table~\ref{tab:rh_oos}) is scored on \rhorDays{} trading days from \rhorSpanStart{} to \rhorSpanEnd.
  \item The empirical analysis focuses on a single equity index (the S\&P~500) and a single target horizon (the 15:30--16:00 ET closing half hour).
  \item In our economic evaluation, the straddle is held unhedged to cash settlement, and executing at quoted bid and ask prices rather than mid-quotes materially reduces the strategy's Sharpe ratio (Section~\ref{sec:rh_econ}).
  \end{itemize}
\item \textbf{Directions for future work.} Natural extensions include out-of-sample evaluation on post-\rhoosSpanEnd{} option expirations, cross-market validation on additional index option chains, and intraday variance forecasting at earlier decision origins prior to 15:30 ET.
\end{itemize}
