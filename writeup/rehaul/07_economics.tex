\item \textbf{Position.} We trade same-day-expiring (0DTE) SPXW options. With $S_t$ denoting the underlying print on the option chain at the 15:30 ET forecast origin, we select the call and put at the nearest adjacent strikes with live quotes:
  \[
  K_C = \min\{K \ge S_t\}, \qquad K_P = \max\{K \le S_t\}.
  \]
  When $K_C = K_P = S_t$, the package forms an at-the-money straddle; otherwise, it forms a narrow strangle on the two strikes bracketing $S_t$. We refer to both positions as the straddle: an approximately delta-neutral package entered at 15:30 ET and held unhedged until expiration at 16:00 ET.

\item \textbf{Entry prices.} From the legs' bid quotes $b$ and ask quotes $a$ at 15:30 ET, we define the package mid-quote $P_t$, bid $P^{b}_t$, and ask $P^{a}_t$ as
  \[
  P_t = \tfrac12\bigl(b^C_t + a^C_t\bigr) + \tfrac12\bigl(b^P_t + a^P_t\bigr),
  \qquad
  P^{b}_t = b^C_t + b^P_t, \qquad P^{a}_t = a^C_t + a^P_t .
  \]
% 2026-10-17 (user, E18): parked -- the close named as the official S\&P 500 close. Was: Settlement in cash at the official close $S^{\mathrm{close}}_t$, ...
\item \textbf{Settlement.} The package settles in cash at the official S\&P~500 close $S^{\mathrm{close}}_t$ with no intraday delta hedging; the simple return on the mid-quote premium paid at entry is
  \[
  X_t = \bigl(S^{\mathrm{close}}_t - K_C\bigr)^+ + \bigl(K_P - S^{\mathrm{close}}_t\bigr)^+,
  \qquad
  R_t = \frac{X_t}{P_t} - 1 \;\ge\; -1 .
  \]
% Code: atm_straddle_lib.py:1725-1741 (settle_package); R = exit / entry - 1 (notebooks/_write_0dte_nb.py:452).
\item \textbf{Implied variance.} Let $\mathrm{IV}^C_t$ and $\mathrm{IV}^P_t$ denote the call and put implied volatilities at 15:30 ET, quoted on a per-hour basis. The implied volatility over the closing half hour is
  \[
  \mathrm{IV}_t = \frac{\mathrm{IV}^C_t + \mathrm{IV}^P_t}{2\sqrt2},
  \]
  and $\mathrm{IV}_t^2$ is the corresponding implied variance to expiration.
% Code: atm_straddle_lib.py:1450-1464 (mean of the two legs, / sqrt 2; NaN if either leg is a censored solver node);

\item \textbf{Forecast variance risk premium.} Following \citet{CarrWu2009} and \citet{BollerslevTauchenZhou2009}, the variance risk premium over the closing half hour is $\mathrm{IV}_t^2 - \E_t[\RV_t]$, which we forecast by $\mathrm{IV}_t^2 - \hat\sigma^2_t$, where $\hat\sigma^2_t$ is the model variance forecast from Section~\ref{sec:rh_methods_setup}. Every trading rule below conditions on the forecast exclusively through the variance gap $\hat\sigma^2_t - \mathrm{IV}_t^2$, taking a long straddle position when forecast variance exceeds implied variance.

\item \textbf{Trading strategies.} A strategy chooses a daily straddle position $q_t$ ($q_t > 0$ long, $q_t < 0$ short) and earns the daily return $r_t = q_t R_t$. \item \textbf{Execution.} At mid-quotes, the strategy return is $r_t = q_t R_t$. At quoted bid and ask prices, buying the straddle pays the ask $P^{a}_t$ and selling receives the bid $P^{b}_t$; because settlement occurs in cash at expiration, the bid--ask spread is incurred at entry only:
  \[
  r^{\mathrm{ba}}_t =
  \begin{cases}
  |q_t|\,\bigl(X_t/P^{a}_t - 1\bigr), & q_t > 0,\\
  |q_t|\,\bigl(1 - X_t/P^{b}_t\bigr), & q_t < 0.
  \end{cases}
  \]
