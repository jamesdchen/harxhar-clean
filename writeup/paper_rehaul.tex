\begin{abstract}
% 2026-10-17 (user, new-notes 1): parked -- the target is built from front-month E-mini futures (03_data, Realized moments). Was: "forecasts of the realized variance of the S\&P 500"
We investigate whether exogenous predictors improve intraday forecasts of a futures-based (E-mini) proxy for the realized variance of the S\&P 500
over the closing half-hour interval (15:30--16:00 ET). Specifically, we estimate each model across all half-hour
intervals and evaluate the forecast issued at 15:30 for the closing half hour over the out-of-sample period from
\rhoosSpanStart{} to \rhoosSpanEnd{} ($T_{\mathrm{oos}} = \rhoosDays$ trading days). We compare a benchmark
heterogeneous autoregressive (HAR) predictor set against an augmented predictor set that incorporates exogenous
series. Under the QLIKE loss, the full predictor set yields lower average out-of-sample loss than the HAR
benchmark across all \rhrAfPoolPairs{} intraday model classes, with \rhrAfPoolLowerSig{} of the
\rhrAfPoolPairs{} reductions statistically significant at the 5\% level, whereas mean squared error does not
confirm these gains. Furthermore, although every intraday specification remains less accurate under QLIKE than a
% 2026-10-18 (user, new-notes 11): parked -- a HAR-type model with its horizons, not a daily HAR regression; still the closing-interval OLS the comparison uses (make_rehaul_results.py:1276). Was: "daily HAR regression estimated solely on the closing interval, incorporating the exogenous predictors narrows"
HAR-type model in the spirit of \citet{Corsi2009}, estimated by OLS on the closing-interval observations of the target's trailing averages over $k \in \{1, 5, 25, 125, 625, 3125\}$ samples plus calendar columns, incorporating the exogenous predictors narrows
this gap across all model classes. In an economic application to zero-days-to-expiration (0DTE) S\&P 500
straddles on expiration days, a sign-based trading strategy achieves a higher Sharpe ratio under the full
predictor set than under the HAR benchmark across every intraday model class.
\end{abstract}
